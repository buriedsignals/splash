The problem

Too much reporting ends up as text.

Comparisons become paragraphs. Places are described instead of mapped. Change over time is written out instead of shown. The internet does not need another wall of words; it needs evidence readers can see.

Visual journalism makes reporting easier to inspect.

In a survey of 768 people involved in data journalism, 70% said data journalism makes stories more reliable and 61% said it helps readers make sense of what they are reading.

Good design helps readers trust the work.

Clear visual design can make the same work feel clearer, more rigorous and more engaging. Readers look for clarity, polish and credible sources when deciding whether to trust a visualisation.

When articles become inputs, visual form becomes identity.

AI turns fixed articles into “liquid information” that machines break apart and recompose. Visual journalism gives the reporting a distinctive form that still belongs to the publisher.

Visuals create another way for the story to travel.

AI Search is multimodal and can use high-quality images and video alongside text. Visuals can become part of how reporting is found and understood.

Splash is an agent system for visual journalism.

You install it into your AI assistant. Splash guides the agent through a research-informed process for understanding the story and data, choosing the right visual form, producing it, and asking for your approval before publication.

How Splash turns reporting into graphics.

Splash guides the agent to understand the story and data, decide what the visual should communicate, and propose forms the evidence supports.

You choose the direction. The agent produces a draft for review, then prepares the approved output for print, web, video or scrollytelling.
