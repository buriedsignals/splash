The case

Four reasons the desk should be showing its work.

Splash is not a case for prettier pages. It is a case for pages that can be checked, trusted, recomposed and found — and each of those is somebody else’s finding, not ours.

0170%
say it makes stories more reliable

In a survey of 768 people working in data journalism, 70% said it makes stories more reliable and 61% said it helps readers make sense of what they are reading.

EJC · State of Data Journalism, 2023
02
Design is read as rigour, before a word is.

Clear visual design makes the same reporting read as clearer, more rigorous and more engaging. A page is judged before it is read, and the judgement carries.

Information Design Journal · ACM CHI
03
When articles become inputs, form becomes identity.

AI turns fixed articles into liquid information that machines break apart and recompose. What survives the recomposition is the shape you gave it.

Reuters Institute, 2025
04
A graphic is a second way of being found.

AI search is multimodal: it carries images and video alongside the text, so a piece with a graphic has more than one way of being found.

Google · AI Search documentation

What Splash does

How Splash turns
reporting into graphics

Splash guides the agent to understand the story and data, decide what the visual should communicate, and propose forms the evidence supports. You choose the direction. The agent produces a draft for review, then prepares the approved output for print, web, video or scrollytelling.
