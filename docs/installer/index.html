Install Atelier

One command sets everything up. You'll enter your keys afterward, in a configurator that opens on your own machine — they never touch this website or your Downloads.

Option A — paste one line into the terminal

Option B — download a file and open it

After it installs, a configurator opens in your browser to collect your keys (MapTiler, Datawrapper, optionally an Anthropic API key). They're verified on the spot and saved to ~/Atelier/.env on your machine.
