# Unemployment rates around the world — find yours

How does your country compare? Hover each bar to read the exact figure.

South Africa posts the highest unemployment rate in this selection at 33.2%, followed by Angola (14.5%) and Afghanistan (13.3%). Most Western economies cluster between 3% and 8%, while Gulf states like Qatar record near-zero rates that reflect structural differences in how labour markets are measured and composed.

Note: figures are not fully comparable across countries — methods of calculation and presentation of unemployment rate vary. All figures are 2024.

**Source:** Wikipedia — List of countries by unemployment rate (figures cited from national statistics and ILO, 2024).
