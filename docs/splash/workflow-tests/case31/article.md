## Brief

Atlanta handled more passengers than any airport on earth in 2024 — 108 million — a dominance US hubs
have held for decades. The top 15 spans 4 continents but US airports take 6 of the 15 slots. Dubai is
the sole Middle Eastern entry at #2. Asia (Japan, China, India, South Korea, Singapore) claims 6 slots.
Europe (London, Istanbul, Paris) holds 3.

The geographic spread of these mega-hubs is the story: a cluster of US domestics, a Gulf connector, an
Asian corridor of rising volume, and a thin European presence — a pattern invisible in a sorted bar
chart but immediately readable on a proportional-symbol world map.
