# Europe's life-expectancy divide runs west to east

Where you are born on the continent still shapes how long you can expect to live. In
2024, people in Spain, Switzerland and Italy could expect to reach about 84 — while in
Bulgaria, Latvia and Romania the figure was closer to 75, a gap of nearly a decade. The
pattern is strikingly geographic: a long-lived Mediterranean and Alpine core, a shorter-
lived eastern periphery.

Source: Eurostat / WHO, 2024.
