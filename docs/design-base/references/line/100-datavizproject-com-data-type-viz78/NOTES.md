# 100 datavizproject — #78, proportional circles clustered by year

- url: https://100.datavizproject.com/data-type/viz78/
- archive: datavizproject
- type: proportional circles, clustered
- export: static
- readAs: the encoding as the page draws it, read at rest

## What it is

The same dataset as two clusters of three circles, one cluster per year, each circle sized by value
and carrying its number.

## What it does with information

**The year is a panel heading in the display register**, set large and bold above its cluster, so
the time dimension is read as a caption rather than as an axis.

**The total sits beneath the cluster**, small and grey — 22 and 33 — so the sum is available without
competing with the parts.

**The circles are packed rather than aligned**, which makes the cluster read as one quantity made of
three, at the cost of any precise comparison between clusters. The printed values are what repair
that cost, and they are what makes the choice defensible.

## What it does with style

House triad, white ground, year headings in bold dark grey at roughly twice the label size.

## What is transferable

- **A year as a panel heading in the display register**, not as an axis tick, when there are two or
  three time points.
- **The total beneath the cluster, in the quiet register** — present for whoever needs it, silent
  for whoever does not.
- **When a packing loses precision, print the values.** The rule generalises: an encoding that
  trades accuracy for gestalt owes the reader the numbers it gave up.

## What is this piece's own

The house palette and the packing geometry.

## What was not verified

Whether circle area or diameter carries the value.
