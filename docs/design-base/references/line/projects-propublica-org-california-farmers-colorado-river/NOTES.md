# ProPublica — "California Farmers and the Colorado River"

- url: https://projects.propublica.org/california-farmers-colorado-river/
- archive: url-list
- type: unit grid (waffle), scrollytelling
- export: scrolly
- readAs: the piece's own graphic element, photographed at rest, with the prose card that sits over
  it at that step

## What it is

The Colorado River's water drawn as a grid of small squares — thousands of them, each a unit of
water — on a pale grey ground, with the article's prose set in a card floating over the grid as the
reader scrolls.

## What it does with information

**A quantity too large to picture is made countable.** A river's flow in acre-feet means nothing as
a number. As a field of squares it has a size the eye can hold, and later steps can take squares
away, colour them, or move them to a claimant — every subsequent argument is a rearrangement of the
same units.

**The prose sits over the graphic, not beside it.** The card carries the sentence the current state
of the grid is meant to prove — "Drought, climate change and growth have taxed the river … But the
water still flows." — so the claim and its evidence are in one field of view rather than in two
columns competing for attention.

**The grid is under-saturated on purpose.** The squares are a pale blue at low contrast against the
grey ground, which reads as *material* rather than as marks demanding to be counted individually;
the emphasis is available for the steps that will need it.

## What it does with style

Ground `#E1E5E6`. Four families in play — Tiempos Headline and Tiempos Text for the serif prose,
Graphik for furniture — with **eleven italic runs and ten letter-spaced runs**, and a type scale
running from 13.3 to 47.8 px across eight distinct sizes. The prose card is a plain white rectangle
with a hairline, no rounding, no shadow beyond a faint edge.

## What is transferable

- **Draw an unimaginable quantity as a countable field of units**, then make every later step a
  rearrangement of that same field. The reader learns the unit once.
- **Put the prose over the graphic, in its own plain card**, when the sentence and the state it
  describes must be read together.
- **Keep the base field quiet.** A unit grid at full contrast spends all its emphasis before the
  argument starts.
- **A serif for the prose against a sans for the furniture**, with italic as a real register rather
  than an accident.

## What is this piece's own

Tiempos and Graphik are ProPublica's licensed faces; the specific pale blue.

## What was not verified

Everything after the step that was read — this is a scrollytelling piece and only one resting state
was captured. Whether the squares are individually meaningful at the reader's actual screen size.
