# The Pudding — "How Artists Get Paid From Streaming"

- url: https://pudding.cool/2022/06/streaming/
- archive: url-list
- type: explanatory diagram, scrollytelling
- export: scrolly
- readAs: the piece's own graphic element, photographed at rest at its first step

## What it is

An explainer on where a stream's money goes, built as a scrollytelling diagram on a deep navy
ground: ring icons for the actors — ARTIST on the left, SPOTIFY, APPLE and STREAMING on the right —
which the following steps connect and animate into a flow.

## What it does with information

**The actors are placed before the flow is drawn.** The first step establishes the cast and their
positions on an otherwise empty ground; nothing is connected yet. A reader arrives at the money
path already knowing what the endpoints are, which is a sequencing decision, not a styling one.

**Identity is a recognisable mark inside a ring**, with a tracked uppercase label beneath. The
Spotify wordmark, the Apple logo: the reader identifies the actor without reading.

**Each actor holds its own hue** — green, red, yellow, blue-violet — assigned by identity rather
than by any quantity, so the later flow can be followed by colour alone.

## What it does with style

Ground `#111044` measured, a deep saturated navy. The marks are **thin luminous strokes** rather
than fills: rings and line art at one or two pixels, glowing against the dark. Display type is set
**light, uppercase and widely tracked**; two families (National 2 Web, Poppins), weights from 300 to
700, and eleven case-transformed runs.

**A limitation of the pixel route, found here.** The palette is read as *sequential, one pole at
241°* — which is wrong: four distinct hues are present. They are thin strokes, so their pixel
coverage is a fraction of a percent against a ground covering most of the frame, and a
coverage-weighted clustering does not see them. **Coverage-weighted palette reading under-reads a
thin-stroke graphic.** The reading kept above is the eye's; the record's `shape` field is not
trustworthy for artifacts of this kind.

## What is transferable

- **Establish the actors before drawing the relation between them** in an explanatory sequence.
- **Hue assigned by identity, held constant through the piece**, so a later flow needs no legend.
- **A dark ground with light, widely tracked display type and luminous thin strokes** is a coherent
  direction, and the furniture derivation this repository already has produces legible ink on it
  without any component naming a literal.

## What is this piece's own

The brand marks, and the neon glow on the display type.

## What was not verified

Everything after the first step. This is a scrollytelling piece and only its resting first state was
read; no claim above depends on the motion, and none is made about it. Contrast floors were not
measured on the navy ground.
