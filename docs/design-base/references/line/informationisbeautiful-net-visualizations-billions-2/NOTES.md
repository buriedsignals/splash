# Information is Beautiful — "$$$Billions"

- url: https://informationisbeautiful.net/visualizations/billions-2/
- archive: informationisbeautiful
- type: treemap
- export: static
- readAs: the published graphic as the page serves it, read at rest

## What it is

A treemap of sums of money at wildly different scales — $25bn for NASA, $708bn for Africa's foreign
debt — each cell labelled with its amount in the display register and its subject beneath.

## What it does with information

**The accent marks a thread, not an extreme.** Most cells are blue; a handful are orange — the Iran
War at $129bn, and "extra revenue for fossil fuel companies due to Iran War raising oil prices" at
$234bn. The orange is not the largest value on the canvas and does not try to be. It picks out an
argument running through the figure, and the reader follows the colour to find it.

That is, precisely, `visual-system.md`'s own rule met in the wild: *"the accent is assigned by the
journalist's answer to 'who is the subject,' never by which value happens to be largest."* A
repeated predecessor failure in this tree was a chart that highlighted its statistical maximum
instead. Here is a published piece doing it right, and it is worth the corpus for that alone.

**Every cell carries its own number**, so the treemap's known weakness — area is hard to compare
across non-adjacent cells — is repaired rather than ignored.

**The label carries its own qualification.** "$225 Mark Zuckerberg — PERSONAL WEALTH (AS OF APR
2026)"; "$129 Iran War — ESTIMATED". The basis of each number is set in a smaller quiet register
directly under it, so no cell states a figure whose kind the reader has to guess.

## What it does with style

Ground `#FDFDFD`, palette read as **diverging**, two poles at 193° and 35°. A blue field, an orange
thread, and a teal-green group at the right edge. Values in the display register, subjects in body,
bases in a small quiet register — three type sizes doing three different jobs inside one cell.

## What is transferable

- **Reserve the accent for the argument's thread, never for the maximum.**
- **Print the value in every cell of an area encoding**, because area does not compare across
  distance.
- **Three registers inside one label** — value, subject, basis — so a figure never appears without
  the kind of figure it is.

## What is this piece's own

The specific hues, and the choice of treemap for quantities that share no common denominator.

## What was not verified

Whether the teal-green cells at the right constitute a third semantic group or are only a tint of
the blue field. The pixel route reads two poles, which suggests a tint; the eye is not certain.
