# ProPublica — "Miseducation: Is There Racial Inequality at Your School?"

- url: https://projects.propublica.org/miseducation/
- archive: url-list
- type: the pair COLLAPSED to a ratio — one derived value per entity, mapped, with both terms named
  in the sentence
- export: interactive (a `RACE` toggle, a four-way `MEASURE` toggle, a search, a zoomable map)
- readAs: the live canvas the page draws, at rest, in its default state (`Black`, `Opportunity`); the
  pixel route measured the canvas element itself (`graphic.png`)

## What it is

A national map of school districts, coloured by **how many times more likely** White students are to
be in an Advanced Placement or gifted programme than Black students. Above the map, a sentence
states the comparison in full; below it, a legend runs `1-2 times as likely` → `5+ times as likely`
in a blue ramp, with three out-of-scale categories placed to its left in their own colours.

## What it does with information

**The pair is collapsed to a ratio and the ratio is the only mark.** Two rates — White and Black —
become one number per district. It is the only way this comparison fits on a map, and it costs the
levels entirely: a district where both groups are well served and one where neither is can carry the
same colour.

**Both terms of the vanished pair are named in the sentence that sits with the graphic**, and the
second term is set in the ramp's own accent: *"School districts where White students are more likely
to be in an Advanced Placement class or gifted and talented program, compared with **Black**
students."* The coloured word matches the `RACE` control directly above it, so the sentence, the
control and the map are one object. Information is Beautiful's *audience vs critics* poster does the
same on a different chart, independently: **when the pair is collapsed, the sentence carries both
terms.**

**The sentence is generated by the controls.** Change `RACE` to `Hispanic` or `MEASURE` to
`Discipline` and the sentence changes with the map. The reader is never left to remember which of
eight states the picture is in.

**The three "no reading" categories are given colours and words, outside the ramp.** `Not Available`
grey, `Less Likely` green, `No Black or no White students enrolled` pink, all set to the LEFT of the
ordered ramp with a visible gap. They are not blanks and they are not the ramp's bottom class; the
legend's own layout says they are a different kind of thing. Measured, `#DFDFDF` is **33.1 %** of the
plate — a third of the map is "no reading", stated rather than hidden.

**Each legend class is labelled with its unit** — `1-2 times as likely`, not `1-2` — so no tick can
be read without knowing what it counts.

## What it does with style

Measured on the canvas (960 × 500, ratio 1.92). Ground `#FFFFFF` at **53.7 %**; the palette reads
**sequential, one cluster at 200°**: `#90BCD2` (1.6 %), `#5498BA` (0.7 %), `#3A7390` (0.4 %),
`#274D60` (0.3 %) — a four-step blue ramp. Neutral furniture is dominated by `#DFDFDF` at **33.1 %**,
the out-of-scale districts, with `#F3F3F3` at 1.5 % and `#C1DAE6` at 1.2 %.

Type, essentially one family — **Graphik** — at four sizes and two weights: 12.6 / 400 for the 493
map and table labels, 12.6 / 400 **capitalize** for 104 more, 10.8 / 400 for the in-map annotations
set in white at 90 % opacity over the plate, 12.6 / 700 for the `State` / `National` toggles, 16.2 /
700 for the headline numbers. **112 case-transformed runs** — by far the most of any reference in
this family — and eight italic runs. One family carrying five roles by size, weight and case alone.

## What is transferable

- **A collapsed pair must name both its terms in the sentence beside the graphic**, and colour the
  compared term to match the control that selects it.
- **Regenerate the sentence from the controls** so an interactive's state is always written down.
- **Set "no reading" categories outside the ordered ramp, with words and a gap.** They are a
  different kind of thing and the legend's layout should say so.
- **Label every legend class with its unit**, not with a bare number.

## What is this piece's own

ProPublica's Graphik; the school-district subject; the Mapbox base and the map itself.

## What was not verified

Whether the ramp's classes are equal-interval or chosen — the labels imply equal steps up to a
`5+` open top class, which the map family's `legend` register would recognise. Whether the underlying
2015-16 Civil Rights Data Collection figures are rates or counts. None of the other seven control
states were opened.
