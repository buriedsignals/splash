# 100 datavizproject — #89, two ranked states mirrored across one shared scale

- url: https://100.datavizproject.com/data-type/viz89/
- archive: datavizproject
- type: one numeric position scale, two time slices hung above and below it
- export: static
- readAs: the encoding as the page draws it, read at rest, in the 1440×900 page screenshot.
  The chart is a raster image inside Ferdio's page shell; the **style route reached the shell only**.

## What it is

A single horizontal scale numbered 0 to 15, drawn as bare numerals with no rule. Above it, the 2004
values hang on thin stems; below it, the 2022 values hang on thin stems. Identity is the national
flag at the end of each stem. Behind each half, the year is set as an enormous pale grey watermark —
`2004` filling the upper band, `2022` the lower.

## What it does with information

**Two ranked states share one scale and are separated by side, not by colour.** Colour is left free
to carry identity (the flags), and time is carried by *which side of the axis a mark is on*. The
reader compares 2004 to 2022 by looking straight up and down a column.

**Movement is drawn as horizontal displacement, not as a line.** Nothing joins an entity's two
marks. The piece asks the reader to find the same flag on the other side of the axis, which is
cheaper for three entities than for thirty, and is the exact trade this shape makes.

**The period label is the ground, not a caption.** Setting `2004` and `2022` as watermarks behind
their own halves means each mark sits *inside* its period. A label that large costs no attention
because it never competes for ink: it is a field, not a line of type.

## What it does with style

Ground: `#F4F7F7` at 48.51 % (the page) against `#FFFFFF` at 41.78 % (the chart card); no graphic
element was isolated. `#3274DA` at 8.60 % is the site's navigation bar. The graphic's own chromatic
contributions are small and identifiable: `#EE5440` at 0.161 % (the Danish and Norwegian flag reds)
and `#EDCB41` at 0.037 %, hue 48°, the Swedish flag's yellow.

The stems are hairlines in a light grey and the scale carries no axis line at all — the numerals
alone do the work of the axis.

## What is transferable

- **Mirror two ranked states across one shared position scale** when colour is already spent on
  identity. Side replaces hue as the time encoding.
- **Set the period as a field behind its own marks**, at display scale and near-invisible value, so
  each half is self-labelling without a caption.
- **An axis can be numerals without a line**, when the marks are stemmed to it.

## What is this piece's own

Three entities. The mirrored form breaks down as soon as the reader has to hunt for a matching mark
across the axis, and with thirty entries it would.

## What was not verified

The graphic's own typography. Which country sits at which value — read off the picture, and no
number is asserted here. Whether the stems are interactive.
