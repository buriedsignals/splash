# ProPublica — "The Toxmap"

- url: https://projects.propublica.org/toxmap/
- archive: url-list
- type: risk-surface map with named facilities
- export: web
- readAs: the piece's own map element, photographed at rest

## What it is

Estimated cancer risk from industrial air pollution along the Gulf coast, drawn as a warm glow over
a dark basemap, with every contributing facility named on the plate.

## What it does with information

**The data labels and the geography labels are two different registers, and never confusable.**
Facility names are set in **white tracked capitals at a very small size** — `EXXONMOBIL BEAUMONT
REFINERY (PART)`, `LOUISIANA PACIFIC CORP` — while the geography keeps its own: the state as
`LOUISIANA` in larger tracked capitals, cities as `Nacogdoches`, `Alexandria` in mixed case. Two
label systems share one plate without a key, because their treatments do not overlap.

**The dark ground buys contrast for a faint quantity.** The risk surface is a soft warm bloom; on a
light basemap it would have had to be far more saturated to read at all. The ground is a decision
about how much contrast the data needs, not a mood.

**Water reads as a blue-teal tint, never grey** — `geo-discipline.md`'s rule 7, met in a published
piece.

**A pill invites the one interaction that matters**: "Click inside a hot spot to see how risks
combine there." Set on the plate, in its own dark chip, rather than in prose above the map.

**An inset locator**, bottom right, the same move as the sibling piece.

## What it does with style

Ground `#333333` measured, palette **sequential**. White type throughout, tracked, mostly capitals,
at two clearly separated sizes.

## What is transferable

- **Two label registers on one plate** — one for the data's own names, one for the geography — kept
  apart by size and case rather than by colour or by a key.
- **A dark plate when the quantity is faint**: it lets a soft encoding stay soft.
- **Put the interaction's instruction on the plate, in a chip**, where the pointer already is.

## What is this piece's own

Mapbox's dark style and its label typography; the warm risk ramp.

## What was not verified

Any interactive state — the resting view alone was read. Whether a legend exists elsewhere on the
page; none is visible in the frame that was captured.
