# ProPublica — "Hate crime reporting, mapped"

- url: https://projects.propublica.org/graphics/hatecrime-map
- archive: url-list
- type: dot map with annotated absences
- export: static
- readAs: the graphic as the page serves it, read at rest

## What it is

Every agency reporting a hate crime to the FBI, one dot per agency, on a pale land silhouette of
the United States. No state borders beyond the outline, no place names at all.

## What it does with information

**The geography is assumed and never labelled.** There is not one toponym on the plate: an American
reader locates Chicago, Houston and Florida from shape alone, and the piece spends none of its ink
on telling them. That is a choice about the audience, and it is only available to a piece whose
readers know the map.

**The argument is carried entirely by four annotations with leaders**, each a short paragraph
curving to the place it concerns — and each about an ABSENCE: "Chicago did report hate crimes, but
many large police and sheriff's agencies around it reported none."; "Hawaii does not report hate
crimes to the FBI at all."; "Jacksonville, the state's largest city, hasn't reported hate crime
since 2016." The dots show what was reported; the annotations show what was not, which no dot can.

**The dot's size carries the count** and nothing else does — no legend, no scale. The finding is
categorical (reported / did not), and the sizes are texture.

## What it does with style

Ground `#FFFFFF` with a pale sand land fill; the pixel route reads the palette as **monochrome** —
black dots, and no second hue anywhere. The annotations are set small, in the same black, with
hairline leaders. A source line sits bottom right.

## What is transferable

- **Annotate the absence.** A map of what was reported cannot show what was not; a short annotated
  paragraph can, and pointing it at a named place makes it concrete rather than statistical.
- **Drop the toponyms when the audience knows the shape** — and only then.
- **A leader that curves** to its target, rather than a straight rule crossing the marks.

## What is this piece's own

The assumption of an American readership, which does not travel.

## What was not verified

The underlying FBI reporting figures. Whether the piece has any interactive state.
