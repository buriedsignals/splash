# Buried Signals — The Yemen Tribute, casualty map

- url: https://yemen-topaz.vercel.app
- archive: buried-signals
- type: proportional-symbol map, filtered by year
- export: web
- readAs: the map behind the piece's own entry screen. Two earlier harvests recorded this site as
  reaching no map at all — the graphic sits behind an "Explore the map" link and the harvester
  scrolled past nothing, because the page is exactly one viewport tall. The record now says
  `entry: "Explore the map"`, because a page read after a door was opened is a page in a state the
  harvester put it in.

## What it is

Civilian casualties from coalition bombings and mines in Yemen, each an open circle sized by count,
on a near-black basemap.

## What it does with information

**The marks are hollow, and that is the whole encoding.** Open circles with a pale stroke overlap
without occluding: where the strikes cluster the outlines pile into a dense white knot, and the
density itself becomes the reading. Filled discs would have hidden each other and lost exactly the
information the piece is about.

**A single year filter along the bottom edge**, set as a row of years with `ALL` selected — the
piece's one interaction, placed on the plate rather than above it.

**The geography is minimal and named sparsely**: `YEMEN` set right, in tracked capitals; a handful
of settlements — `Jizan`, `Assab`, `Aden` — in mixed case; governorate borders as hairlines. The
country is not filled; the sea and the land are separated by a coastline alone.

## What it does with style

A near-black ground with white and olive marks, and a condensed tracked capital face for the title
and the furniture. Everything that is not a casualty is grey.

## What is transferable

- **Hollow marks when overlap carries meaning.** Density read through outlines is information that
  filled marks destroy.
- **A dark plate for a piece about loss**, where the marks are the only light on it.
- **Put the one filter on the plate**, at the edge, rather than in a control strip above.

## What is this piece's own — and why it cannot corroborate

**Buried Signals' own work.** Splash is being built with that house; using it to corroborate this
tool's own vocabulary is the parochialism the evidence floor refuses. Filed because it teaches. See
`METHOD.md`, correction 9.

## What was not verified

Every filtered year but `ALL`. Whether the place labels were restyled or left as the provider
delivered them — the same limit recorded for La Nación and for the sibling Gaza piece.
