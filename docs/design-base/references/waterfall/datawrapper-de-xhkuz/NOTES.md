## What it is

A time bridge: an opening cash balance of $7.5B in 2016, nine annual changes, and a closing `Total`
of $13.6B. The categories are years, so the sequence is fixed by the calendar.

## What it does with information

**A tinted band marks the era the headline names.** 2016 through 2021 sit on `#F4F9FC` at
**29.06 %** — a barely-there blue wash covering nearly a third of the plate — and 2022 onward sit on
white. The chart's two-clause title (built up … spent it down) is drawn as two backgrounds before
any bar is read.

**Prose annotations live inside the band, in ink, with a mixed weight.** `The Nintendo Switch was
released in March 2017` and `2021 sales were driven by COVID-19 lockdowns, Animal Crossing's
success, and peak Nintendo Switch sales` — Roboto 14/400 with the subject in 14/700 and one word in
italic. They occupy the empty space under the small early bars, which a bridge that starts low
always has.

**Opening and closing totals are grey and start at zero.** `#C4C4C4` at 6.799 % is the two full-height
bars; the steps are `#30B8E2` at 3.411 % (increase) and `#E6735D` at 1.448 % (decrease).

**Signed labels outside the bar in the bar's own colour**, with magnitude formatting that follows the
number rather than the column: `+$567M`, `+$855M`, `+$1.4B`, `+$5.2B`, `−$1.2B`. The two totals are
`$7.5B` and `$13.6B` in black.

**The x-axis is years and the last category is `Total`** — a label that is not a year sitting in a
row of years. The chart accepts that small inconsistency rather than inventing a 2026.

## What it does with style

Ground `#FFFFFF` at **52.24 %** with the band `#F4F9FC` at 29.06 % — together 81.3 % of the frame;
the lowest white reading of the four Datawrapper records here, entirely because of the band. Palette
read as **diverging**. Ink `#000000` 1.241 %, `#181818` 0.661 %; gridlines `#EBEBEB` 0.492 %.

Type inside the frame is Roboto: 22/700 title, 15/400 subtitle (`Nintendo annual cash on hand
between 2016 and 2025`), 13/400 for 29 label runs, 14/400 and 14/700 for the annotations, 11/400
source.

## What is transferable

- **Tint the background under the stretch of steps a clause of the headline is about.** It costs no
  ink, no colour and no legend, and it is readable before the bars are.
- **Put the prose in the hole a rising bridge leaves.** A bridge that starts low has a large empty
  bottom-left, and it is the only chart shape that reliably does.
- **Format each delta at its own magnitude** (`$567M` next to `$5.2B`) rather than padding a column.
