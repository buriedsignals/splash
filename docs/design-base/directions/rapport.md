## Ground and accent

- `ground` — the reference's own graphic sits on `#E1E5E6`, a pale grey; the page behind it is
  effectively white. `#FFFFFF` is taken as the direction's ground, and the reference's pale grey is
  recorded as its own choice of a quiet field for a unit grid rather than as this direction's paper.
- `accent: #1F5C8B` — a step deeper than the reference's own pale unit blue, which is deliberately
  under-saturated as a base field (see the reference's note). An accent must carry emphasis; the
  reference's field colour is chosen precisely not to.
