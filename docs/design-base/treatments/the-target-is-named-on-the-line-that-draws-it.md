## The rule

Write what the line is, on the line.

## The two publications

The BBC's election plate sets `326 seats for a majority` on the rule that draws it. This base's
record of it: *"the whole apparatus: no legend, no caption, no lookup."* ICAEW names each threshold
beside the segment it belongs to — `to 3.5 % of GDP` — so the words do the work a legend entry
would, next to the thing they are about.

## Why it matters more here than elsewhere

A bullet's second mark is the one a reader has no other way to identify. A bar is obviously a
quantity; a thin rule across it could be a target, an average, a previous year, a forecast. Unnamed,
it is a mark the reader must be told about somewhere else — and "somewhere else" on a static plate
means a caption they may not have.
