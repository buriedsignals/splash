## The rule

Add a mark. Do not overwrite a meaning.

## The two publications

Information is Beautiful emphasises one mark *"with an outline and a note rather than by recolouring
it, so it keeps its category"*. Reuters fades the population rather than dropping it and marks the
subject *"with a ring and a label, so emphasis costs no encoding channel"*.

## What recolouring costs

On a plate where fill already means something — a category, a sign, a side of a break — recolouring
the subject spends a channel that is carrying information, and the reader loses the subject's own
class in exchange for knowing which row to look at. A ring is the cheapest emphasis available: it
adds a mark instead of taking one away.

## Where this base already disagreed with itself

Several beats in this tree mark their subject by giving it the accent while the rest take the
neutral — and that is compatible, because on those plates the fill carries nothing else: one series,
one accent, no category to lose. The rule bites where the fill is already spent, which is exactly
where the temptation is strongest.
