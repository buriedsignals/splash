## The rule

A map's labels are not one register. Administrative area, settlement and water are three classes of
thing, and a reader should be able to tell which is which **without a legend, from typography
alone**.

## The evidence, and why this pair is the one that counts

Two publications, at two desks, on two continents, reaching the same three-way split independently.
The harvest record for the SCMP piece says it outright: *"that is the same three-treatment convention
ProPublica's `louisiana-toxic-air` uses on an entirely different continent, at an entirely
independent desk. Two publications, and it is the pair that finally evidences the `place` register."*

**South China Morning Post** — the country as `CHINA`, tracked capitals, grey, the quietest thing on
the map; the feature under discussion as `Yangtze River basin`, mixed case, in the piece's orange
accent, with a hairline leader to the outlined basin; the water as `Yangtze River`, italic, in the
river's own blue.

**ProPublica** — parishes as `EAST BATON ROUGE`, `ST. JAMES`, grey tracked capitals; cities as
`Baton Rouge`, `Gonzales`, `New Orleans`, darker mixed case; water as `LAKE PONTCHARTRAIN`,
`MISSISSIPPI RIVER`, italic capitals.

Both also put the same third thing in the accent: the feature the story is about. Colour is doing the
job the typography is doing, in the same direction.

## Where the three treatments come from

**They are derived, not filed.** No direction in this base carries a `place` row: all three were
measured on pieces that are not maps, so a `place` register would be a value nothing had measured —
the same reason `hairline` is derived from `rule` and `muted` from the ground.

So a plate builds the three out of registers the direction DID file, keeping the corpus's own
distinctions:

- **area** — the `axis` register's family and size, uppercased and tracked, in the muted ink. The
  quietest of the three, and the one both records set that way.
- **settlement** — the `annot` register, mixed case, one step darker than the area. Both records
  make the settlement the more legible of the two administrative classes.
- **water** — the `annot` register in italic, in the water tint (`water-is-a-tint-not-a-grey`).

A direction that later files its own `place` rows overrides the derivation, exactly as a filed
`hairline` overrides its.

## The fourth thing, which is not a place

The feature the story is about takes the accent and a leader. It is not a fourth class of place — it
is the beat's subject, wearing the same clothes it wears on every other form in this base.
