## The rule

The field and the figure do different jobs, and the plate needs both.

## The evidence

**Information is Beautiful**: *"the magnitude is stated twice, in two registers — as a headline number
in the display register (`Over 1.6 Million Casualties`) and as a countable field of marks. **Neither
alone would do the work**: the number is unreadable as a quantity, the field is unreadable as a
figure."*

`100.datavizproject.com`'s viz30 pairs its countable rail with printed endpoints and a delta.

## The second half, which is a rule of its own

viz30 states its delta *"in units, not percent — **the honest register for a countable rail**"*, and
checks it: 5→8 is +3, 4→10 is +6, 13→15 is +2. *"Both readings are true; the encoding chooses one and
the label agrees with it."*

A field of things is counted in things, so its own figure is a count. A percentage printed beside a
countable field asks the reader to hold two arithmetics at once, and the one they can check by eye is
not the one the label gave them.
