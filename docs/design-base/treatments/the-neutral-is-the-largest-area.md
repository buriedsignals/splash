## The rule

Colour the argument. Everything else is grey — and on this form "everything else" is most of the
picture.

## Measured on all three

LLNL makes rejected energy grey, and grey is **11 %** of the picture: the design's biggest colour
decision is to make the waste colourless. The IEA's `#A1A1A1` covers **5.31 %** against a largest
hue at **4.25 %**. Carbon Brief runs **16 % neutral ribbon against 1 % accent**.

## The sentence worth keeping

*The categorical cap is respected not by having few colours but by making sure the many colours are
small.* On a form whose ribbon mass is enormous, that is the only arrangement under which an accent
means anything at all.

## The related choice this does not settle

Whether the hue TRAVELS with a source through the stages (the IEA) or sits on the nodes with neutral
ribbons (Carbon Brief) is a second decision, and the two publications answer it differently. The
condition is legible: colour can travel only where a reader could actually follow one ribbon across
the plate.
