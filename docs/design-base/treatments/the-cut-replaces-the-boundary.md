# the-cut-replaces-the-boundary

- kind: imported
- name: Splitting into panels does the work a boundary line, a tint or an extra axis would do
- applies: the beat draws two or more panels
- draws: axis
- priority: 7
- evidence: datawrapper-de-oingq
- evidence: 100-datavizproject-com-data-type-viz99
- detect: the delivered plate draws no boundary between panels beyond the gap itself

## The rule

A panel split is furniture **removed**. Splitting and then drawing the boundary you split to avoid
pays the same cost twice.

## The evidence, from opposite directions

**Datawrapper** cuts a year into twelve month blocks: *"the month is made visible by cutting the grid,
not by drawing a boundary … the cost is that the week straddling two months is split; the gain is
that no boundary line, no alternating tint and no month-tick axis is needed at all."*

`100.datavizproject.com`'s viz99 splits a grouped column into panels and says what the gap then
means: *"the between-group gap is no longer a gap; it is **the end of one axis and the start of
another**."*

## What it costs, and both records name it

Datawrapper's cost is a straddling week cut in two. viz99's is that each panel's baseline has to be
drawn — a short rule exactly as wide as its own pair — because a gap that means "new axis" has to
look like one. Neither is free; both are cheaper than the furniture they replace.
