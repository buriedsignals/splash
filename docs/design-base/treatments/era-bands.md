# era-bands

- kind: derived
- name: Datable events inside the series' own span are shaded
- applies: the beat declares events whose dates fall inside the series' own extent
- draws: eyebrow
- priority: 1
- detect: the delivered artifact carries one flat, low-opacity rect per declared era, spanning the
  plot's full height between that era's own x coordinates, each with a text run in the eyebrow
  register
- provenBy: proof/co2-suisse/renders/*.png

## The rule

Shade the span of each declared event as a flat band behind the marks, labelled in the eyebrow
register.

## Why it needs no published precedent

The events are the beat's own declared data, like its reference level: the treatment draws what the
beat carries and imports no practice.

## What it does

**It puts the explanation where the shape is.** A series that dips in 1974 and 1980 explains itself
once the oil shocks are drawn on it; without them the reader either knows the history or does not.

**Flat, never a gradient.** `visual-system.md`: if the ground must communicate something it does so
as a flat fill with its own legible label, never as a decorative wash — "any gradient or texture on
the ground competes with the marks for the reader's contrast budget."

## What limits it — measured

Two era labels **collided** the first time this was drawn beside a peak label: "CHOC PÉTSECOND
CHOC". The bands were also narrower than their own labels. Both are the arbiter's problem now, and
this treatment's low priority is why it is the one dropped when the corner is crowded. A band whose
label cannot be placed is still drawn; only the label goes.
