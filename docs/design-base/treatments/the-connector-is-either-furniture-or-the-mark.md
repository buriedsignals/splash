## The rule

Decide what the connector is. A rule that is neither furniture nor mark is a third thing competing
with both.

## Two publications, two answers, and one of them names the fork

Information is Beautiful: *"the connector is furniture and the endpoints are the marks"* — a rule
thinner and lighter than either dot, which *"groups the pair without becoming a third mark, and that
is what lets a hundred pairs sit on one plate"*. By coverage it is still the largest non-ground ink
on that plate, which is what furniture at scale looks like.

`100.datavizproject.com`'s viz19: *"the connector is NOT furniture here — it is the mark that carries
the change"*, with the delta set **inside** it, because the connector already spans exactly the
change.

The IiB record names the choice itself: *"both are coherent."*

## The condition

When the two LEVELS are the story and the rows are many, the connector recedes. When the CHANGE is
the story, the connector carries it and the number goes inside it. A beat that cannot say which of
those it is has not decided what it is about.
