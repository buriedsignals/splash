- kind: imported

- draws: value

- detect: every pair whose two states nearly coincide carries its own printed value

## The rule

Write the number the drawing cannot show.

## The two publications

The Pudding: *"write a value that is too small to draw rather than letting the reader see nothing."*
Reuters: *"draw the no-change case in neutral and give it its numbers."*

## Why it is not a nicety on this form

A pair whose two states nearly coincide draws one dot with a smudge beside it, and that reads as a
**missing reading** rather than as a small change. The printed number is the only thing separating
"barely moved" from "not measured" — and on a plate where every other row shows a visible span, the
reader's default assumption is the wrong one.
