## The rule

An opaque pile is a picture whose truth is a function of iteration order. That is a property of the
loop that drew it, not of the data.

## Two answers, two publications, two families

**Translucency**, when the marks are areas and the cluster should read as TONE. Carbon Brief draws
its flows as translucent strokes so *"bundles darken, and nothing depends on which link was drawn
last"*; the IEA is the same answer at 0.6 alpha, where *"crossings resolve into visible density
instead of an arbitrary z-order."*

**Hollow outlines**, when the marks are countable objects and the cluster should read as a NUMBER OF
THINGS. Buried Signals' Yemen map: *"the marks are hollow, and that is the whole encoding. Open
circles with a pale stroke overlap without occluding: where the strikes cluster the outlines pile
into a dense white knot, and the density itself becomes the reading. **Filled discs would have hidden
each other** and lost exactly the information the piece is about."*

## Its relation to the flow rule already filed

`ribbons-are-translucent-so-crossings-are-honest` is this rule for one geometry, with one of the two
answers. This is the general form, and it is filed separately because the second answer is not
available to a ribbon and the condition that chooses between them is not visible from inside the
sankey family.

## A note on the evidence

The `map` family's two proportional-symbol references are both Buried Signals — **one publication**,
which is below this base's floor. This rule clears the floor only because a sankey record says the
same thing from another desk. Where a family's own corpus is one newsroom, a beat in it draws on the
base's general rules; it does not get family rules of its own until a second desk is harvested.

## The condition the corpus does not state, and this base measured

Hollow marks stay countable only while the cluster is countable. Yemen's field is a few hundred
strikes; a field of 8,900 circles on a continental camera fuses, and the rule is obeyed while the
plate is unreadable.

The measure is the **worst cell** of a grid over the frame — the stroke length of every mark whose
centre falls in it, against the cell's own area — because an average over the whole frame hides
exactly the failure a dense field has. Measured on
`proof/static-proportional-symbol-europe-capacity`: 8,900 circles gave a worst cell of **302 %** of
its own area in outline while the whole map read 13 %. A beat drawing a field of hollow marks owes
that measurement and a floor; that beat's is 40 %, and it reaches it by drawing fewer marks and
saying so.
