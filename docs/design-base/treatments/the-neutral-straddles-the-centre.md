## The rule

The undecided mass sits ON the anchor, symmetric about it.

## Why it is a claim and not a layout

Vega-Lite's record: *"the neutral is a third thing — achromatic grey, belonging to neither ramp, and
straddled across the zero, so 'which way does this row lean' is answered by which side is longer,
with the undecided mass symmetric about the anchor."*

Pushing that mass onto one side is not a tidier drawing. **It adds the neutral's whole length to that
side's lean**, silently, on every row — and the lean is the only thing this form is for. The FT's own
specimen carries the same arrangement, which is what makes it a rule rather than one example's habit.

## What "neither side" has to mean

A factual classification, not a judgement. On the beat this was filed from, the centre is nuclear
generation: neither a fossil fuel nor a renewable, which is a definition rather than an opinion. A
neutral chosen because the designer finds a category awkward is a thumb on the scale.
