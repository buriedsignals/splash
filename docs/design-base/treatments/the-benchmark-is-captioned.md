## The rule

A normalised radius with no stated population is not a measurement. Say what it is a share OF.

## The two publications

The Analyst captions it in a sentence under the plate: *"Percentile comparison vs. top five European
league forwards over the last 15 years (1,350+ minutes)"*. StatsBomb puts the same information on
the plate as furniture — minutes played, competition, season, age and date of birth, and the
template name (`Striker Radar and Distributions`) that says which population the percentiles are
against.

## Why it survives leaving sport

Of everything this family does, this is the rule with nothing sport-specific in it. A percentile is
against a population; a share is of a total. The beat this was filed from draws each country's
generation mix as shares of **that country's own generation**, which is what makes its nine axes
commensurable at all — and a reader who does not know that denominator is reading nine unrelated
lengths.
