## What it is

The register a map sets its own geography in — countries, administrative areas, settlements, water.
Not the data's labels, which are a different voice and must stay separable from it.

## Where it was seen, and why these two

**ProPublica, *Louisiana Toxic Air*.** Parishes in grey tracked capitals (`EAST BATON ROUGE`,
`ST. JAMES`, `ASCENSION`); settlements in darker mixed case (`Baton Rouge`, `Gonzales`,
`New Orleans`); water in italic capitals (`LAKE PONTCHARTRAIN`, `MISSISSIPPI RIVER`).

**South China Morning Post, *China's worst floods in decades*.** The country as `CHINA` in tracked
grey capitals; the named feature as `Yangtze River basin` in mixed case, in the piece's accent; the
water as `Yangtze River` in **italic**, in the river's own blue.

Two continents, two desks, and the **same three treatments in the same roles**. That is what makes
this a practice rather than a house habit — the distinction this floor exists to draw.

## What is transferable

Administrative area in tracked capitals, settlement in mixed case, **water in italic**. A reader
separates the three without a key, because the convention is old enough that they already carry it —
which is the only kind of resonance worth spending on
(`palette/references/subject-conventions.md`).

## Why it derives from `annot`

A place name is the map speaking about what the reader is looking at, which is what an annotation
is. Where a direction records no `place` — because the piece it was measured on was not a map — the
register is taken from `annot` at 0.88×, and the derivation is reported.

## What was refused along the way, and why it matters

Two other maps name their geography and neither was accepted:

- **La Nación, *Mapa del delito*** — its labels are the basemap PROVIDER'S defaults, left as
  delivered. At that resolution a default cannot honestly be told from an editorial decision.
- **Buried Signals, Gaza** — our own house. It teaches; it cannot corroborate.

The register waited two harvest waves and 28 candidates for a second desk that had actually
*decided* its geography. It is filed now because SCMP did.
