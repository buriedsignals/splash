## What it is

The register a graphic sets its scale's own labels in — tick values, their unit, the years along a
time axis. Quiet enough to recede behind the marks, present enough to read a value against.

## Where it was seen

Our World in Data's per-capita CO₂ chart sets its y ticks as "25 t", "20 t", "15 t" — **the unit on
every tick**, not once in a corner — and its x ticks as bare years ending on 2024, the last reading
rather than the next round number. Ferdio's `viz1` sets its own scale labels in the same quiet
register beside its stacked columns.

Two publications, so this is a practice rather than one desk's habit.

## Why it derives from `body`

A scale label and running text share a voice: both are the graphic speaking plainly, neither is the
subject. Where a direction records no `axis` — because the piece it was measured on had no scale —
the register is taken from `body` at 0.88×, and the derivation is reported so nothing inferred looks
measured.

## What is NOT filed here, and why

`legend`, `place` and `step` were in the code before this record existed, invented from a reading of
this repository's own map component rather than from any published piece. **The corpus holds no map
reference at all** — fifteen references, not one a map — so nothing evidences what a published map
calls its apparatus. They are removed until a map family is harvested and the references say.
