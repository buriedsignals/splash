01 · The evidence

Visual journalism makes reporting easier to inspect.

In a survey of 768 people involved in data journalism, 70% said data journalism makes stories more reliable, and 61% said it helps readers make sense of what they are reading.

Source · EJC State of Data Journalism Survey, 2023
02 · Trust

Good design helps readers trust the work.

Clear visual design can make the same work feel clearer, more rigorous and more engaging.

Source · Information Design Journal · ACM CHI, 2024–25
03 · The AI shift

When articles become inputs, visual form becomes identity.

AI turns fixed articles into “liquid information” that machines break apart and recompose.

Source · Reuters Institute, 2025
04 · Discovery

Visuals create another way for the story to travel.

AI Search is multimodal, and can use high-quality images and video alongside text.

Source · Google AI Search documentation
