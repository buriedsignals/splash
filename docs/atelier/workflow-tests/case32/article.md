# 2024 Box Office: The Year's Biggest Films

Inside Out 2 dominated the 2024 global box office with $1.70 billion, making it the highest-grossing film of the year by a wide margin. Deadpool & Wolverine followed with $1.34 billion, while Moana 2 crossed the $1 billion threshold. The top ten were dominated by sequels and franchise entries.
