# UK Private Rent — December 2025 Bulletin

**Source:** ONS, *Private rent and house prices, UK: December 2025*
URL: https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/bulletins/privaterentandhousepricesuk/december2025

---

## Brief

The ONS December 2025 private-rent bulletin reports average monthly private rents and year-on-year
percentage changes across the four UK nations and selected English regions (November 2025 data,
except Northern Ireland which reports September 2025).

The headline is a cross-geography ranking story: private rent inflation is slowing nationally
(from 5.0% to 4.4%), but the pace varies sharply — the North East (8.4%) runs at three times
the rate of London (2.8%), even though London's absolute rent (£2,271/month) is three times
higher than the North East (£759/month).
