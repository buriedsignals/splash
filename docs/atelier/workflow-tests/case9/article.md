# Solar power's uneven global rise

Solar has gone from a rounding error to a mainstream source of electricity — but very
unevenly. In 2024, a handful of countries drew a fifth or more of their power from the
sun: Chile, sun-soaked and grid-constrained, led at around 22%, with Spain, Greece and
Australia close behind. Much of northern Europe and the industrial giants of Asia and
North America still sat in the single digits or low teens.

The pattern is not simply "sunny south, cloudy north" — policy and grid economics matter
as much as latitude. But shown on a world map, the leaders stand out sharply from the
long tail of countries only beginning their solar build-out.

Source: Ember Global Electricity Review 2025.
