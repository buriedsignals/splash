The answer recorded for this beat: the white ground and the colour the Danube's route is drawn in.

`origin: journalist`, for the same reason its video sibling records: `matchConvention` fires on
"river" and offers blue, and blue is what the basemap already gives the Danube — a route in the
basemap's own water colour is a route a reader cannot pick out. Declining a convention on purpose
is a journalist's decision.

**The route's colour moved, and the measurement is why.** This beat drew the route in Okabe–Ito
orange `#E69F00`, which on this white ground measures **2.25:1** — under the 3:1 floor WCAG 2.2
SC 1.4.11 sets for a mark a reader identifies data by, and the route is the only mark on this plate
that carries the story. `twin-palette` would never have recommended it; nothing measured it,
because until 2026-08-10 nothing between the proposal and the render did. Recorded instead:
`#C68900` at **3.01:1** — not an invented colour, but `adjustToContrast` walking this beat's own
orange toward the ink pole and stopping at the first step that clears, the same arithmetic the
refusal prints beside the failure. The render moves: the route and its legend swatch go one step
deeper. The argument the old comment made — hold this hue back from the territory cycle so the
route never wears a country's colour — is untouched and still true.
