# Recycling rates across Europe

Germany recycles more of its municipal waste than any other country on the continent, at 67.8 per cent. At the other end, Macedonia recycles under a fifth. The figures come from the national environment agencies, collected in March 2025, and the agencies do not all use the same definition of "recycled" — some count material sent for sorting, others only material that actually re-entered production.
