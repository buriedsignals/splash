# Approved — 1-life-expectancy-2023

The rendered beat was opened at 1600×900, 1024×768, 768×1024 and 375×667 and looked at, and the
live layer was driven separately with a real key at 1600×900 and 768×1024.

What was checked, and what it showed:

- The claim the title makes is the claim the joined data makes: six drawn countries under 60 years,
  all six named, Nigeria the lowest of the 229 drawn. `checkClaim` refuses to render otherwise.
- Every number a reader receives — the title, the legend caption, the two notes, the alt text — is
  built from the join, not typed beside it. The counts say 229, which is what is drawn, never 237
  or 241.
- Sub-Saharan Africa reads as the darkest band on the map at a glance, and Haiti and Nauru are
  visibly low outside it, which is the texture the claim flattens and the reference asks to keep.
- The map fits every window it was opened in, nothing scrolls inside the visual, and the plate's
  aspect is never distorted.
- Hovering Nigeria on the live map answers "Nigeria : 54.5 years". Tab reaches all 241 regions in
  value order, starting at Monaco — the reading the picture physically cannot show.
- With JavaScript off the whole beat renders from the baked plate: map, legend, both notes, caveat.

Two things a journalist should know before this is published, and both are in the caveat on the
graphic: the number is not a forecast, and eight territories the source reports separately have no
shape at this scale and are drawn nowhere.

Decision: approved for delivery as an interactive web embed.
