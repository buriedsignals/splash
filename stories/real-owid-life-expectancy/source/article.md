# Life expectancy, country by country

The Our World in Data life-expectancy series, drawn from Riley (2005), Zijdeman et al. (2015), the
Human Mortality Database (2025) and the UN World Population Prospects (2024). 265 entities.

What the number means, from the dataset's own description:

> Period life expectancy is the number of years the average person born in a certain year would
> live if they experienced the same chances of dying at each age as people did that year.

That definition matters for how we caption it: it is not a forecast of how long a baby born today
will actually live.

We want a map on the site — the reader should be able to hover a country and read its figure. One
year, the most recent the data supports properly. Choose that year yourself and tell us why.

Source: Riley (2005); Zijdeman et al. (2015); HMD (2025); UN WPP (2024) – with major processing by
Our World in Data.
