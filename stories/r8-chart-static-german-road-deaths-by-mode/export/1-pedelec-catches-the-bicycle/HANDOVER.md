## The one thing this does not show

You named this limit yourself, and it belongs beside the visual — in the caption or the
paragraph next to it — not only in your notes.

> This counts deaths, not risk. The German road-accident statistic records no distance ridden, so nothing here says a pedelec is more dangerous per kilometre than a bicycle — only that more people are being killed on one. Both series begin in 2014 because that is when the statistic began counting pedelecs separately; a single bicycle series drawn across 2013 and 2014 would show a change that is a definition, not a road. And the 16.4 per cent your piece quotes is the April preliminary figure: the July table this chart is drawn from gives 462 cyclists of 2 832 road deaths, which is 16.3 per cent.
