# Europe's low-carbon fleet is still water and the atom — but ten countries have tipped

Europe has about 469 GW of low-carbon power capacity installed, according to the WRI Global Power Plant Database. Most of it is still hydro and nuclear: France alone, with its reactors, holds the largest share.

But the picture is shifting at the edges. In 10 European countries, wind and solar already make up more than half of the low-carbon fleet: the United Kingdom, Portugal, the Netherlands, Ireland, Denmark, Estonia, Belarus, and — on the southern shore of the Mediterranean that the database counts with Europe — Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia. Together they hold 14 % of the continent's low-carbon capacity.

The figures count installed capacity, not the electricity actually generated, and the database does not list every small station.
