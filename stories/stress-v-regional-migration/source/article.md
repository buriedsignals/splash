# Three regions are emptying while the coast fills up

Net migration in 2025 was negative in four of the seven regions. Centre lost 21,800 people on balance and Nord lost 12,400, while Ouest gained 15,600 and Sud 8,900. Montagne was almost exactly level, losing 780.
