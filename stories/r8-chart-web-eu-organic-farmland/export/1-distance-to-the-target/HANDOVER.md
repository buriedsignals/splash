## The alt text

Paste this as the image's alternative text. A reader using a screen reader gets the finding,
not a description of a chart.

> A horizontal lollipop chart. One row per European Union member state, each drawn as a stem from zero to that country's organic share of its utilised agricultural area, with a dot at the end. A vertical rule at 25 % marks the target the EU set for 2030. The top block holds the 25 member states that published a figure for 2024, ranked from Estonia at 22.6 % down to Malta at 0.8 %; not one of their stems reaches the rule. Below a dividing line sit the 2 member states with no figure for 2024: Austria, 25.7 % in 2020, Greece, 12.2 % in 2021. Austria's stem is the only one on the chart that crosses the rule. Every row's own year, Eurostat flag and change since 2015 are available on hover, on tap and to a keyboard, and the table below this chart prints all of them in the same order.
