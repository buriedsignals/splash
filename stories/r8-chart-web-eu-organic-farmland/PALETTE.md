# The palette for this story

`palette`'s proposal (`proposePalette`, with the parsed `NEWSROOM.md` profile and this beat's own
`chart/web` pair, which grounds every ratio on the newsroom's screen ground) offered two options and
recommended the first:

| option | accent | contrast on `#16191B` | verdict |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Buried Signals's house colours | `#D4A853` | 8.01:1 | passes, **recommended** |
| Buried Signals's house accent 2 | `#5B8A8A` | 4.58:1 | passes |

No subject convention was offered, and the proposal said so out loud: organic farmland carries no
colour a European reader already holds the way blue carries water or green carries renewable
generation. Green would have been the obvious reach and it would have been a decoration, not a
convention — so the house leads, which is the rule when the subject carries nothing.

The recommendation is taken. `#D4A853` on `#16191B` is the widest passing margin of the two, and
this beat draws thirty thin stems rather than a handful of large marks, so margin is worth having.

The chart spends exactly one accent. The stems and their dots carry it. The 25 % target rule, the
axis, the country labels and the year stamps are furniture, derived from the ground. A country whose
most recent published year is older than the newest year in the table is NOT given a second colour —
it keeps the same accent at a reduced opacity and states its own year in words beside it, because a
second hue would read as a second category and this chart has only one.
