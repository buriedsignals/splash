# China's emissions now outweigh the next five emitters combined

In 2024 China emitted 12.3 billion tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry. The next five
emitters — the United States, India, Russia, Japan and Indonesia — emitted 11.7 billion tonnes
between them, so China alone put out more than all five together.

The figures are national territorial emissions; they do not count emissions embedded in traded
goods, and they leave out land-use change.

We would like a short video for social channels that makes the comparison visible.
