# Europe's coal is a two-country problem

In 2024, France, Germany, Norway, Poland, Sweden and Switzerland generated 1,637.5 TWh of electricity between them. Coal was 12 % of it — 200.9 TWh.

Almost all of that coal — 99.5 % — was burnt in two countries, Germany and Poland. France's coal was 0.2 % of its mix; in Norway, Sweden and Switzerland it was zero.

The figures cover one year and six countries chosen for contrast, not Europe as a whole.
