# Richer countries live longer, until they don't

Across the 165 countries with both measures in 2021, income and life expectancy rise together, but not forever.
Beyond $30,000 per person, life expectancy fits in a band 3 times narrower: below that line, 124 countries spread
over 41 years of life expectancy; above it, 41 countries fit within 14 years.

The relation is a correlation, not a cause. Health systems, conflict and disease move independently of income, and
the poorer countries' wide band is where those differences show.

Data: World Bank via Gapminder, UN WPP (2024), via Our World in Data.
