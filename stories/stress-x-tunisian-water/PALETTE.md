# The colours this story is drawn in

The subject is water, and blue for water is the one convention this toolchain's own
`SUBJECT_CONVENTIONS` table calls "the single most reliably held colour association in the
semantic-resonance study". `#1F6FB2` is that table's own value, not a colour invented here.

`palette`'s `proposePalette` did **not** offer it. Its conventions match English and French
words only, so the recorded subject — `محافظة تونس`, in the story's own language — matched nothing
and the proposal came back with the house colours alone, recommending `#D4A853`. The answer was
given through the proposal's third branch ("something else"), with the convention's own hex.

Measured on this ground before it was written down: `#1F6FB2` against `#16191B` is **3.34:1**,
above the 3:1 non-text floor. The two house accents are kept in `accents` for any later beat that
needs a second and a third series; both clear the floor too (`#D4A853` 8.01:1, `#5B8A8A` 4.58:1).

`origin: subject` is the honest value: the colour is the subject's convention, and a journalist
chose it over the house recommendation.
