# استهلاك المياه في المحافظات التونسية

تستهلك محافظة تونس أكثر من غيرها من المياه، بواقع 142 مليون متر مكعب في السنة.

Water consumption is highest in Tunis governorate at 142 million cubic metres a year, but Tunis is also the most populous. Sfax, the second largest consumer, has almost as many residents. The figures come from the national water utility and cover the 2025 calendar year.
