# Cleaner at home, lighter in Europe

Between 2000 and 2024, all 16 European countries in this dataset cleaned up their own electricity: every one of them
now draws a larger share of its power from low-carbon sources, renewables plus nuclear.

Yet France lost 11.8 points of weight in Europe's low-carbon total. It gained 4.2 points at home, but the others
built low-carbon power faster, so its share of the group's output fell from 38.4% to 26.5%. Five of the 16 countries
weigh less in the group than they did in 2000.

Data: Ember, Energy Institute – Statistical Review of World Energy (2025), via Our World in Data.
