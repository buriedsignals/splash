A world choropleth of 194 countries shaded by the number of human rabies deaths each reported to WHO
for 2024, in 6 classes from 0 to 250 and over. 94 countries filed nothing and are painted in a
neutral no-reading fill, including India, China and Pakistan. The darkest countries are Afghanistan,
which reported 641, the highest of any country, together with the Philippines, Ghana and South Sudan.
The 100 countries that did file reported 3 021 deaths between them, against WHO's standing estimate
of about 59 000 a year.
