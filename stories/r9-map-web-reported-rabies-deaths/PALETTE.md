The answer recorded for this beat: Buried Signals's own ground and primary accent, chosen from the
two options the proposal put up.

`origin: newsroom` — the proposal read the subject as *human rabies deaths reported to WHO by
country, 2024* and said plainly that no convention applies to it. The grounded conventions are
renewables, fossil, water and heat; none fires on a mortality register, and inventing one would be a
colour nobody could read a quantity off.

Why the amber and not the house teal. This beat is a SIX-CLASS choropleth, so the accent is not one
ink, it is the top of a ramp that has to put six distinguishable steps between the ground and
itself. The proposal measured `#D4A853` at **8.01:1** against `#16191B` and `#5B8A8A` at **4.58:1**.
The teal clears the 3:1 floor for a single mark and would still have been a legitimate answer for a
one-accent beat; for six classes it is the shorter runway, and the classes at the bottom of a teal
ramp would sit on top of each other. The amber was chosen for the headroom, not for the mood.

The surface was NOT stated to the proposal, so every ratio above is measured against `#16191B`, a
SCREEN ground. This beat is delivered as an HTML page and never as paper, so that is the right
destination — but it is the proposal's own caveat and it is recorded rather than dropped.

The water and the no-data fill are decided by this file too. `basemapWaterFor`, `waterFor` and
`noDataFor` derive them from the pair above rather than from a literal; the measurements the bake
and the render read back are recorded in the beat's own `NOTES` entry and in `JOIN.json`.
