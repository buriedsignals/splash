**That recommendation is declined here, for a reason specific to this beat's own chart design.**
The data does not survive being drawn as a part-to-whole (see `BRIEF.md`, "The trap" — the six
shares sum to 95.2, not 100, and Imports is negative), so this beat is not six wedges or six
stacked bands one per source; it is a diverging bar with ONE accent used to encode SIGN, not
category — every positive share in the same ink, the one negative share (Imports) in the
furniture's own muted. Applying the `renewables` green to that accent would colour Hydro, Nuclear
and Other identically, which teaches the reader the green-means-renewable code this table exists to
invoke and then breaks it on the same chart: Nuclear and Other are not renewable generation, and a
reader who already holds that association would misread them as such. A convention borrowed for a
purpose it does not fit is worse than no convention, so the newsroom's own house colours lead
instead, exactly as they do whenever no convention genuinely applies.
