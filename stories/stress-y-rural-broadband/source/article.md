# Fast broadband still stops at the edge of town

Across 186 municipalities surveyed in June 2025, household broadband coverage ranges from under a third to almost complete. The five regions differ sharply: Ouest and Centre are consistently above the national picture, Sud consistently below it.

The survey returns are not clean. A handful of municipalities returned no figure at all, and one — Commune-063 — returned a coverage figure above 100 per cent, which the agency has not yet explained.
