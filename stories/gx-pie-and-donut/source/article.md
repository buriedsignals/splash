# A third of the world's CO2 comes from one country

The world emitted about 37 billion tonnes of CO2 from fossil fuels and industry in 2023. China emitted 32.9 % of it — 12.2 billion tonnes.

That is more than twice the United States, the second emitter, at 13.3 %. India follows with 8.3 %.

These are territorial emissions: they do not count the CO2 embodied in goods a country imports. The totals are per-person emissions multiplied by population.
