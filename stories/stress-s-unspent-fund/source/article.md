# One fund, and the € 4.1 billion it has not spent

The regional resilience fund set up in 2024 has disbursed nothing. Of the € 4.1 billion allocated to it by the national recovery plan, € 0 had reached a beneficiary by the end of June 2026 — a figure the fund's own quarterly return confirms and its director did not dispute when we put it to her last week.

There is nothing else to show. There is one fund, one allocation, and one disbursement figure, and the disbursement figure is zero.

Give us the strongest possible visual treatment of that fact for the top of the piece.
