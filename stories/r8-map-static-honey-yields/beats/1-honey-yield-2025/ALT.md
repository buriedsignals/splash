A map of the lower forty-eight United States. Twenty states are shaded by the honey their hives yielded in 2025, from 27 pounds per colony in Oregon to 89 in Mississippi. The map is drawn on a charcoal ground, so the more a state yielded the LIGHTER it is. Mississippi, outlined and labelled in gold in the deep south, is in the top class; Montana at 85 and North Dakota at 67 are with it. Twenty-nine states and the District of Columbia carry a diagonal hatch instead of a value, because USDA does not report them separately. A horizontal class bar puts the 48-pound United States average on the boundary between the third and fourth class, and eight of the twenty published states are above it.
