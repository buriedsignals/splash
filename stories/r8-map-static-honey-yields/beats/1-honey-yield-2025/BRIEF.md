# Beat — Mississippi's hives made 89 pounds of honey each (static)

**Type:** choropleth. **Medium/format:** map / static. **Producer:** custom (`map-beat`).
**Language:** en. **Channel:** one PNG, `renders/honey-yield-2025-still.png`, 1920 × 1080.

## What this beat proves

That Mississippi's reported yield per colony in 2025 — 89 pounds — is roughly double the 48-pound
United States average and higher than any of the other nineteen states USDA reports on its own; and,
at the same time, that most of the map carries no value, because USDA reports twenty states and
pools the rest.

Both halves are the argument. A map of this release that showed only the twenty would be a map of
the United States with thirty states quietly missing.
