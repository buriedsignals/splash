# The Rhône glacier has lost two thirds of its area since 1990

Measurements taken every five years by the Swiss glacier monitoring network show the Rhône glacier shrinking from 1.82 square kilometres in 1990 to 0.61 in 2025 — a loss of two thirds. The volume has fallen further still, from 0.094 cubic kilometres to 0.019, because the ice has thinned as well as retreated.

The retreat has not been steady. Between 2000 and 2005 the recorded area did not change at all, which the monitoring network attributes to a run of heavy winters rather than to any recovery; the loss resumed immediately afterwards and accelerated after 2010.
