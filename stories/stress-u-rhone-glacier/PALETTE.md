# Colours for this story

`proposePalette({ newsroom, subject })` was run with the subject read as *the Rhone glacier: ice
area and volume, water*, and printed three options with `formatProposal`. Recorded here is
**option 1, the one it recommended**: the water convention.

- Ground `#16191B` — kept from `NEWSROOM.md`, Buried Signals's own.
- Accent `#1F6FB2` — `skills/palette/references/subject-conventions.md`, the water row. Blue for
  water is the convention a reader already holds, and a glacier is water.
- Measured: **3.34:1** against the ground, above the 3:1 non-text floor. It is the thinnest of the
  three options offered — the house gold `#D4A853` reads at 8.01:1 and the house teal `#5B8A8A` at
  4.58:1 — and that is worth knowing when this palette is reused for a beat whose accent has to
  carry small type rather than a stroke.

The other two options, both measured and both passing, were Buried Signals's house colours
(`#D4A853`, 8.01:1) and its second house accent (`#5B8A8A`, 4.58:1).

`origin: subject` because the subject's own convention is what was chosen, not the newsroom's brand.
