# How much of the world's electricity is renewable now

We have Ember's renewables share of electricity generation, as published by Our World in Data,
covering 246 entities from 1900 to 2025. The dataset's own description is plain about what the
number is:

> Measured as a percentage of total electricity produced in the country or region.

Source line, verbatim from the file's metadata: *Ember (2026) and other sources – with major
processing by Our World in Data.*

The desk wants this on the site as something a reader can move through — pick a country, see where
it sits, compare it against others. Not a static picture. It goes next to a piece about grid
investment, so the comparison between countries is the point, and the reader should be able to find
their own.

One thing the newsroom already knows and has asked us not to smooth over: several countries report
through more than one body, and the totals do not always agree. If that shows up in this file, say
so on the graphic rather than picking one quietly.
