# Europe set itself an antibiotics target for 2030. It is moving the other way.

In 2023 the Council of the European Union told member states to cut human antibiotic
consumption by a fifth. Five years into the eleven-year run to that deadline, the EU is
consuming more antibiotics than it did at the starting line.

The measure is a mouthful — defined daily doses (DDD) of antibacterials for systemic use,
per 1 000 inhabitants, per day — but the arithmetic is not. The European Centre for Disease
Prevention and Control (ECDC) put it in one sentence in its Annual Epidemiological Report
published on 18 November 2025:

> "In 2024, the EU population-weighted mean total consumption (community and hospital
> sectors combined) of antibacterials for systemic use (ATC group J01) was 20.3 DDD per
> 1 000 inhabitants per day (country range 9.8−29.9). This was 2% higher than in 2019
> (baseline year), and 4.4 DDD per 1 000 inhabitants per day higher than the 2030 target
> of 15.9 DDD per 1 000 inhabitants per day."

The 2023 Council Recommendation on stepping up EU actions to combat antimicrobial
resistance sets the target as a 20% reduction on the 2019 level by 2030. The 2019 level was
19.9. Twenty per cent below it is 15.9. Nothing since has moved towards it.

The pandemic briefly did what policy has not. Consumption fell to 16.4 in 2020 and stayed
there in 2021, close enough to the target to look like an achievement. It was not one: the
European Environment Agency, which tracks the same indicator on its zero-pollution
dashboard, records that "a temporary reduction was noted during the COVID-19 pandemic years
of 2020 and 2021 ... after which consumption rebounded back to pre-pandemic levels", and
that "as poor progress has been made so far, the EU is unlikely to meet this target by 2030
without considerable efforts."

By 2022 the line was back at 19.4. By 2024 it was at 20.3 — above where it started.

The national picture behind the average is wide: the Netherlands used 9.8 DDD per 1 000
inhabitants per day in 2024, Greece 29.9, three times as much. But the target is set on the
EU population-weighted mean, and that is the number the deadline will be judged on.

## Data

Eurostat, `sdg_03_70`, *Consumption of antibiotics in the community and hospital sectors —
defined daily doses (DDD) per day*, dataset updated 5 March 2026, downloaded 23 August 2026.
The series is compiled by ECDC through ESAC-Net. Values carry Eurostat's own flags: `:` for
data not available, `b` for a break in the time series, `d` for a differing definition.
The row `A,P_THAB,EU27_2020` is the EU aggregate and sits in the middle of the country rows.
