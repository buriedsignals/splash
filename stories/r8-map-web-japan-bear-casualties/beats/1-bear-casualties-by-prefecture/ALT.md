A map of Japan with one circle per prefecture, sized by the number of people hurt by bears in fiscal 2025. The circles cluster along the northern spine of Honshu: Akita draws by far the largest at 67, with Iwate beside it on the Pacific coast at 40, and Fukushima, Niigata, Nagano, Yamagata, Gunma and Aomori around them. Western and southern Japan is almost bare, and eight prefectures in the far south-west carry no circle at all because the ministry does not list them.
