## What the visual shows

One line per region, left dot at its 2020 school count, right dot at its 2026 count, region names
set in Greek beside their own line. Attica's line is nearly flat; the steepest lines belong to
Western Macedonia, the Peloponnese and Central Greece. Anatoliki Makedonia kai Thraki keeps its
2020 dot and left-hand label but carries no line and no right-hand dot -- its own 2026 figure is
corrupted in the frozen source and is declared missing directly on the chart, not guessed at.
