## The one thing this does not show

You named this limit yourself, and it belongs beside the visual — in the caption or the
paragraph next to it — not only in your notes.

> These are counts of detected fire EVENTS, not area burned and not people affected. Most of Africa's count is seasonal savanna and agricultural burning rather than the kind of wildfire the explainer quoted in the article describes, so this chart should not be captioned as a measure of wildfire harm. The counts are not comparable between countries of different size, and this dataset carries no area or population figure to divide by. 2026 is excluded because the year is incomplete: the series was last updated on 21 August 2026, so its 370,394 fires are about eight months against fourteen full years. The nine aggregate rows in the source table (World, the six continents, European Union (27), Europe excluding Russia) are not countries; any country ranking taken from this file has to drop them first.
