A scroll-driven page in four steps: a dark map of Switzerland carrying 1,406 dots, one for every avalanche since the winter of 1936/37 that killed somebody, coloured gold where the victims were in buildings or on transport routes and teal where they were on tour or off-piste; then a diagram of those two terrains; then a chart of the two counts across 88 winters, in which the gold line falls from a 1950/51 peak of 99 deaths to near zero while the teal one does not; then the avalanche danger level the national bulletin had forecast on the 323 fatal avalanches that carry one, where level 3, considerable, holds 209.
