Electricity, 2024

Europe's most low-carbon power grids are far ahead of the rest

Top ten ≥ 86% · the rest average 55%

In 2024, forty European countries generated electricity from a mix of fossil fuels, renewables and nuclear power. Ranked by how low-carbon each grid was, one group stands far apart.

Every one of the ten leaders — from Albania down to Austria — generated at least 86% of its electricity from hydro, wind, solar, bioenergy or nuclear power. Albania and Iceland reached 100%.

Below them, the drop is sharp: the other 30 countries average just 55%, and 12 of them — down to Moldova — generate less than half their power without fossil fuels.

Source : Ember / Our World in Data, electricity generation by source, 2024. Low-carbon = hydro, wind, solar, bioenergy, other renewables, nuclear. Ukraine excluded (no recorded generation in this file).
