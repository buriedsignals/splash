# Most people here earn far less than the average

Payroll data for 240 employees shows a median annual salary well below the mean, because a small
number of very high salaries pull the average up. Three individuals earn more than four times the
typical figure for their department, and six returns were blank.

The union says quoting "the average salary" in negotiations is misleading and wants the spread
published instead. We agree — show the distribution, not one number.
