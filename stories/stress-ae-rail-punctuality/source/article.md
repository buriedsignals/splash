# More passengers every year, and later trains every year

Rail passenger numbers rose from 58.2 million in 2014 to 74.6 million in 2025, with the pandemic
year as the one break in the series. Punctuality fell across the same period, from 91.4 per cent to
81.6 — except in 2020, when almost nobody travelled and 94.6 per cent of trains ran on time.
