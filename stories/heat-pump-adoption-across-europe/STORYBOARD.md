## Slot 1 — The gap that persists

The slope chart shows all ten countries as lines from 2021 to 2025. Every line slopes upward — the universal increase is visible at a glance. The vertical spread between the endpoints remains wide: Norway and Sweden at the top, the UK and Spain at the bottom. The angle carries direction; the spread carries the gap. One mechanism, both halves of the finding.

### Candidates considered

1. **Slope (slopegraph)** — chosen. Direction and gap in one frame; the slope angle IS the universal increase, the vertical spread IS the persistent gap.
2. **Dumbbell (range plot)** — gap size per country, sortable. Quieter comparison; loses the directional sweep.
3. **Line** — full 5-year trajectory. Shows the path, but 10 lines crowd the two-point argument the article makes.
