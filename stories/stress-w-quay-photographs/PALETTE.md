# The palette for this story

`palette`'s proposal (`proposePalette`) found no subject convention for "the quay, its fishing
fleet and its crane, 1994 to 2025" — none of the grounded conventions in
`references/subject-conventions.md` fires on a harbour — so the newsroom's own house colours led.

Both recorded house accents were measured against the house ground and both clear the 3:1 non-text
floor (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11): `#D4A853` at 8.01:1 (the proposal's own `recommended`) and `#5B8A8A`
at 4.58:1.

**What an image beat actually spends these on.** Almost nothing. `image-beat` draws no marks: the
evidence is three photographs, and `references/image-discipline.md` is explicit that the closed
palette governs the furniture this skill invents, never the colours inside a photograph. So
`ground` carries the frame, `deriveFurniture(ground)` derives the title ink, the caption ink, the
muted credit grey and the `grid` that fills the letterbox bars — and **the accent is recorded and
unused**. It is recorded anyway so that a later beat on this story, or a scrolly that carries this
one, takes the newsroom's own colour rather than inventing one.

No journalist was present to answer this proposal interactively. Per `skills/palette/SKILL.md`,
"When nobody is there to answer," the proposal's own `recommended` option is recorded verbatim and
`origin: newsroom` names where it came from rather than claiming a journalist answered.
