# Thirty years on the same quay

Three photographs of the same stretch of quay, taken in 1994, 2010 and 2025. The 1994 picture shows fishing boats moored three deep. By 2025 the berths are empty and the crane has gone.

The middle photograph came from the archive without a caption or a photographer's name, and nobody at the paper can now say who took it. We should still run it — it is the only picture of the quay from that decade.

Run all three as a sequence at the top of the piece.
