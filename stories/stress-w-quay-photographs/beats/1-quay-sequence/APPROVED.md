# Beat 1 — approved

**Gate 3.** The artifact was surfaced and looked at:
`stories/stress-w-quay-photographs/beats/1-quay-sequence/renders/still.png`, 1920 x 1080, the size
`BRIEF.md` pins and the size the delivered PNG's own IHDR measures.

Approved with the three caveats below recorded rather than fixed, because two of them are the
format's and not this beat's.

1. **The letterbox bars are drawn in `grid`, which measures 1.75:1 against this newsroom's ground.**
   Geometrically the fit is exact — the 1994 frame (3:2) is 568 x 379 with 149 px bars above and
   below, the 2010 frame (3:4) is 504 x 672 with 32 px bars each side, the 2025 panorama (8:3) is
   568 x 213 with 233 px bars. Visually, on a dark ground, a dark photograph and its own bar are
   1.15:1 apart and a reader cannot see where the 2025 photograph ends. Nothing in `image-beat`
   measures `grid` against anything, and `image-discipline.md` forbids the hairline frame that
   would fix it.
2. **One consistent box costs the panorama two thirds of its height.** That is the discipline
   working as written — letterbox, never crop — and it is also the reason the "empty berths" frame,
   which is what the argument turns on, is the smallest picture on the page.
3. **The typeface is `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`, and nobody chose it.** `TYPEFACE.md` records
   the answer; this format has no `useTypeface`, so the record does not reach the render. Named in
   the hand-over.

Two of the three photographs carry no photographer and print `Source: not stated`. That is the
desk's own decision, recorded in `STORYBOARD.md`'s `limits` and printed on the artefact.
