In 1980 the lime kilns at Alcanede employed 1 860 people across 42 active kilns. In 2026 the site employs 18, and 1 kiln is still firing.

Each interval takes a bigger share of what is left than the one before it: 67% of the kilns still standing in 2010 were gone by 2020, and 67% of those left in 2020 by 2026. In kilns lost per YEAR the fall is slower now than it was in the 1990s — 1.2 a year between 1990 and 2000 — because there is so little left to lose.

The Alcanede kiln yard, photographed in 1980. 42 kilns were firing that year and 1 860 people worked here.

There is no number for what a working yard looks like, which is why this frame is in the sequence at all.

The same yard, photographed in 2026. 1 kiln, 18 workers, 46 years later.

The two frames are the same rectangle, so the comparison is the reader's own — nothing here measures anything from a photograph.

The site sits at 39.74°N, 8.81°W, in the limestone country between Leiria and Santarem.
