# One kiln left

The lime kilns at Alcanede employed 1,860 people across 42 active kilns in 1980. One kiln is still
firing today, with eighteen workers. The decline was steady until 2010 and then steepened.

We have two photographs of the site, one from 1980 and one taken this year, and we know where the
site is. Tell it as a scrollytelling piece — the chart, then the two photographs, then the map — so
the reader arrives at the last kiln.
