# Render audit — 2026-08-09

Item 9 of the owner's list: **re-verify every render, old and new.** The previous audit covered 46
artifacts and found 11 defective. This one covers the whole tree.

**Snapshot:** `experiment/doctrine-twin`, artifacts as committed around `6044a492`. Other agents were
writing into this tree throughout (the web seeds under `twin/skills/chart-web/` and
`twin/skills/map-web/` were modified mid-audit; several map `BRIEF.md` files were *created*
mid-audit; `twin/proof/palette-proof/` appeared mid-audit). Where that matters it is said in place.

**Nothing in this audit was fixed.** It is a report only.

## 1. What was actually opened

The tree holds **109 rendered artifacts**: 70 PNG, 20 self-contained HTML, 19 mp4.

| Kind | In tree | Opened |
|---|---|---|
| PNG | 70 | 70 |
| HTML | 20 | 20 (rendered in real Chrome at 1600 / 1440 / 1024 / 375 px) |
| mp4 | 19 | 19 (frames extracted **from the mp4 itself**, never from the supplied still) |

Of the 70 PNGs, 16 are committed intermediate frame-stills inside three video folders
(`map-quake-symbol/render/frame-*.png`, `mapgen-choropleth-video/render/frame-*.png`,
`mapgen-flowmap-video/render/frame-*.png`). For those the audit worked from **freshly extracted
frames of the parent mp4** rather than trusting the committed copy — which is the stricter check, but
means their verdict is inherited from the parent video rather than established independently. That
distinction is recorded rather than glossed.

**Method, as the project's doctrine requires.** Every artifact was opened and looked at. Every
assertion — title, subtitle, annotation, axis tick, legend, footnote, alt text, tooltip, credit — was
recomputed from the beat's own frozen data with Python or bun, never taken from `BRIEF.md`, the
component, or the title. Interactive files were driven with **real pointer events at measured
coordinates** (`page.mouse.move` / `touchscreen.tap`), never `.focus()`. Where a sentence carried
three claims, all three were checked.

**Result: 43 artifacts are defective** (49 counting the 6 committed frame-stills that inherit their
parent video's defects). **17 carry a false claim delivered to a reader.**

### F1 — Eight comparison PNGs render a fabricated series, credited to a real institution

`twin/proof/comparison/3-MIGRATION--{twin,twin-d3,main-datawrapper,main-chartnative}.png`
`twin/proof/comparison/2-VIE--{twin,twin-d3,main-datawrapper,main-chartnative}.png`

Timeline: the PNGs were rendered 2026-08-08 13:54; the two data corrections landed 2026-08-09
04:36/04:37. Neither the images nor the dossier were re-run.

This is the project's own named worst class — *a credit naming an institution that does not publish
the figure shown* — live in eight artifacts.

### F3 — The hex-density map draws the world 1.58 times, and the extra copies are empty

`twin/proof/map-quake-density/render/static.png` · fix at `map-quake-density/bake.mjs:96-104`

`fitBounds` on an 836×300 box is height-limited: 138° of latitude into 300 px makes the world
**528.03 px** wide, but the frame is 836. MapLibre's default `renderWorldCopies` fills the surplus.
Proven two ways — columns 0–307 of the plate are pixel-identical to columns +528 (mean abs channel
diff 0.16/255), and an independent projection reproduces all 14,044 geometry points at worldSize
528.03 to 0.0 px.

**Verified by eye on the delivered PNG:** the hex grid stops at both edges while land continues. The
Americas render at the right edge with **no hexagons at all** — a reader reads "no earthquakes on the
American Pacific coast", which flatly contradicts the title's *"the Pacific 'Ring of Fire'"*. Add
`renderWorldCopies: false`, or bake a plate whose aspect matches the bounds (≈836×475).

### F12 — The scrolly's alt says "tap" works; tap is broken

`twin/proof/weby-small-multiples-co2-per-capita/small-multiples-co2-per-capita.html`

Alt (read by screen readers): *"Every one of the 300 annual readings … is available on hover, **tap**
or keyboard focus."* Driven for real at 375×900 with touch emulation, the captured sequence is
`pointerdown` (tooltip shown) → `pointerup` → `pointerleave` (**tooltip cleared**) → `touchend` →
`focus`. Final state: `hidden = true`, `display: none`, `focusable = false`. **On any touch device
this beat has no tooltip at all.**

Root cause: `.hit-area` is painted on top of the `.pt` circles with `pointerEvents="all"` and **no**
`tabIndex`, so it swallows hit testing and cannot itself take focus. The other four `weby-*` beats
survive the identical `pointerleave`-after-touch pattern only because their hit rect *is* the
focusable element and the trailing `focus` re-shows the tooltip — a coincidence, not a guard.

Fix: `small-multiples-interaction.mjs:116` (guard `evt.pointerType !== "touch"`, or give the hit rect
`tabIndex`); overlay at `SmallMultiplesCo2Web.tsx:423-432`; the false alt string is emitted from
`render-web.mjs:49`. The pattern is present in all five: `boxplot:59`, `pyramid:60`, `lollipop:50`,
`dumbbell:62`, `small-multiples:116`.

## 7. What this says about the guards

The six mechanical guards did their job on the classes they cover. Everything above sits in the gaps:

1. **Prose is still the unguarded surface, and it got worse, not better.** Every F15–F17 defect, the
   `COMPARISON.md` list, the four off-by-one BRIEF row counts, and the `migration` comments that
   preserve the pre-fix false numbers verbatim — none is reachable by a structural scan. The map
   `BRIEF.md` files written *during* this audit already carry numbers that do not reproduce.
2. **A hand-typed claim beside a derived one is the recurring shape.** `more-heatmap`'s title
   hard-codes `7.8 / 7.5 / 3.6 / 3.8` while the script computes those very values and only
   `console.log`s them. `weby-small-multiples` gates the overtake but types the peak years.
   `weby-lollipop` gates the rank but not the "less than half" clause, whose margin is 1.2%.
   `vidy-heatmap`'s title is derived while its conclusion callout beside it is a literal. All correct
   today; none would go red if the data moved.
3. **A tripwire looser than the claim it protects is not a tripwire.** `vidy-lollipop` guards `> 30`
   for a title claiming 31. `vidy-boxplot` has no guard on its 4× headline at all.
4. **Two fixes exist in one copy and not the sibling.** The struck-through-label fix
   (`LifeExpectancyVideo.tsx:177` records catching it) never reached `EmissionsWeb.tsx`. The legend
   `wrap()` exists in `QuakeSymbolVideo.tsx:145` and not in `QuakeSymbolStill.tsx:256`. The histogram
   tick bug is in both the static and the web copy.
5. **A guard that is never passed is inert.** `renderScrolly`'s `proseLane` reservation exists,
   is documented, and `mapmore-scrolly-danube` neither passes nor honours it.
6. **The comparison dossier's evidence directory is stale by design of nobody.** Eight PNGs predate
   the corrections that made their claims false, and `COMPARISON.md:502-505` asserts that everything
   in it was verified directly.

The single highest-value guard suggested by this audit: **a beat's rendered furniture strings must be
derived from the frozen data or asserted against it.** Twelve of the seventeen false claims here are
a literal sitting where a computation belongs, which is the same finding the tree already recorded
for the previous 55 beats — the class was diagnosed but never mechanized.
