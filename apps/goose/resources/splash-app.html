Credential setup

Splash reads keys from its process environment. Supply them through your agent configuration, a secret manager, or a private .env file stored wherever you choose.

We recommend keeping the file outside the checkout. Ask your agent to load it when starting Splash, then restart Splash whenever you change its values. To open the studio directly from the checkout:

bun --env-file=/path/to/.env apps/goose/studio/open.mjs

Open a Splash story

Choose the story folder your agent prepared. Splash reads its article, data and saved decisions to show the next visual choice.

Starting a new story?

Give your article and data to your agent and ask it to start a Splash story. The agent creates the story folder and prepares the material for your visual decisions. Open that folder here when it is ready.

An empty folder or a folder of raw files is not yet a Splash story.

Suggestions use your story’s saved data profile. Unavailable options show what needs to be connected or completed.
