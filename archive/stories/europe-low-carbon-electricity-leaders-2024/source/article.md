# Europe's low-carbon electricity leaders, and the gap behind them

In 2024, a small cluster of European countries generated almost all their electricity from
low-carbon sources — hydro, wind, solar, bioenergy, other renewables and nuclear combined. Norway,
Iceland and Albania sit above 95 percent. But the leaderboard drops off fast: by the tenth country,
low-carbon share is already several points lower, and by the twentieth it has fallen much further,
with several countries still under half.

The data (Ember/Our World in Data, 2024 generation by source) lets us rank every European country by
low-carbon share of generation and show exactly how far the top ten are ahead of the rest of the
continent.
