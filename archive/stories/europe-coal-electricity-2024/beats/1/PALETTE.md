# The colours this story is drawn in

Derived from `NEWSROOM.md`, which preflight validated, and measured on write against the same non-text contrast floor `parsePalette` measures on read. Nobody was asked, because there was nothing here to decide: no subject convention applies and the newsroom's own pair clears the floor.

A journalist who wants a different colour for one beat drops a `PALETTE.md` beside that beat —
`readPalette` walks up from the beat's own directory and finds the nearest one first.
