Hydroelectricity is Europe's oldest renewable, and in 2024 it is anything but evenly spread. Five
countries with the rivers and mountains for it -- Russia, Norway, Turkey, France and Sweden --
generate almost two-thirds of everything the continent's hydro plants produce; the other
thirty-plus countries in this panel share the remaining third, several of them close to nothing at
all.

A cartogram, resized on each country's own share of the 885.9 TWh total, makes that unevenness
legible as shape: a country's own weight grows or shrinks with what it actually contributes, so a
reader sees the concentration directly rather than reading it off a ranked bar list. Choreography:
open on ordinary Europe (undistorted), morph to the hydro-weighted cartogram, then hold on the top
five picked out by colour with the read-off number carried in the card.
