# Europe's hydropower runs through a handful of countries

Hydroelectricity is often described as Europe's oldest renewable, but in 2024 it is far from
evenly spread across the continent. A small group of countries with the right rivers and
mountains — Norway, Turkey, Sweden, Switzerland, Italy, France, Austria — account for the large
majority of the hydro Europe generates, while many countries generate little or none.

This piece asks: which countries does Europe's hydro actually come from, and how concentrated is
that supply? For a reader in a country with almost no hydro capacity, the answer matters — it
shows how exposed the continent is to the fortunes (rainfall, snowmelt, drought) of a small set of
places.
