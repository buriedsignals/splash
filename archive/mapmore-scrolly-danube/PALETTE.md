The answer recorded for this beat: the white ground and the colour the Danube's route is drawn in
at every step of the scroll.

`origin: journalist`. `matchConvention` does fire on "river" — blue for water is the single most
reliably held colour association this project records — but blue is what the BASEMAP already gives
the Danube, and a route drawn in the basemap's own water colour is a route a reader cannot pick
out. Declining a convention on purpose is a journalist's decision, not the newsroom's and not the
subject's.

**The route's colour moved, and the measurement is why.** This beat drew it in Okabe–Ito orange
`#E69F00`, which on this white ground measures **2.25:1** — under the 3:1 floor WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11
sets for a mark a reader identifies data by, and on this plate the route IS the story. Recorded
instead: `#C68900` at **3.01:1** — not an invented colour, but `adjustToContrast` walking this
beat's own orange toward the ink pole and stopping at the first step that clears, the same
arithmetic the refusal prints beside the failure. Its two sibling flow-map beats
(`mapmore-flow-danube`, `mapgen-flowmap-video`) record the same value for the same reason.
