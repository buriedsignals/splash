# Bullet — in video

Worked example: `proof/video-bullet-low-carbon-share` (validated 2026-09-14), from `proof/static-bullet-low-carbon-share`.

- **Two states of one measure are one hue at two chromas**: the thick pale bar is the earlier date, the thin saturated bar
  the later; a key of two swatches says which. The later bar appears at the earlier bar's end and extends on from it —
  never from zero — each gain counting in a column of its own.
- **The gesture only a video has is re-sorting in front of the viewer**: the rows start in the order of the earlier state
  and re-sort by gain as an insertion computed in Bun — one row at a time climbs to its slot while the rows it passes step
  down one slot together; a step that moves nothing is not a step. The climbing row is drawn over the rows it passes, its
  words haloed.
- A reference line (« la moitié ») drops at the end and every row but the subject steps back.
- No zoom onto the saturated end unless the title makes that claim.
