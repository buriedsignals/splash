# Gantt — in video

Worked example: `proof/video-gantt-top-ten-tenure` (validated 2026-09-14), from `proof/static-gantt-top-ten-tenure`.

- **The gesture only a video has is running the clock**: every bar grows year by year together, linear in years, so a span
  that stops is seen stopping and an interruption is seen as a gap opening — the span labels the still needs become
  unnecessary.
- **A count that falls is the claim happening**: « {n} jamais sortis » counts the entities of the first year that have
  held every year the clock has fully crossed; the name of the one that left is muted at the very frame the count drops,
  so the viewer ties the drop to its row. Entities that were not there at the start are muted from the start — they were
  never counted.
- **The filter comes after the clock**: the rows the title is about keep the accent, the rest steps back. Nothing is
  picked out before.
- Rows keep the still's order (first year, then tenure); the count sits over the rows, no plate; the ticks under them.
