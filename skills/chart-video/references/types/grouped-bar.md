# Grouped bar — in video

Worked example: `proof/video-grouped-bar-wind-vs-solar` (validated 2026-09-14), from `proof/static-wind-vs-solar`.

- **The gesture only a video has is running the comparison one group at a time**: the first series rises across every
  group, then the second rises beside it group by group, each bar eased (an arrival) and counting its value; a count
  of the groups where the claim holds climbs only as each second bar lands.
- **The exception is found by the count stopping**, then kept alone while every other group steps back — no callout.
- **Every bar labelled lets the axis go.** A value wider than its bar is refused in Bun: print it bare and say the unit
  once, after the series' names (« ■ Éolien ■ Solaire · en % de l'électricité »), each name after a swatch of its bars
  so the unit does not read as part of the last name.
- The two values of one group must not touch: side by side, or apart by a line's height — measured at their final heights.
