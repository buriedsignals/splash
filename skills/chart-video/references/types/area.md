# Area — in video

Worked example: `proof/video-area-swiss-co2` (validated 2026-09-14), from `proof/static-area-swiss-co2`.

- **The gesture only a video has is the fill**: the surface advances through time, linear in years, closed on zero — its
  surface is the stock — while the running total counts up; every total's text is measured in Bun.
- **The split** is the subject: a rule at the year the stock reaches its half, the earlier surface stepping back to a
  tint, each half named inside itself with its years and its share on two lines, centred in its own span and refused if
  the span or the surface cannot hold it.
- The years are asserted consecutive before anything is drawn: an area closes silently over a gap.
- The running total and the credit stand in the empty upper left, under the top gridline.
