# Line — in video

Worked example: `proof/video-line-swiss-co2` (validated 2026-09-14), from the static `proof/co2-suisse`.

- **The gesture only a video has is the trace**: the line draws itself through time, linear in years (equal frames, equal
  years — easing would lie about the pace of the data), the year and its reading riding at the tip, the text for every
  year measured in Bun.
- **The reference is furniture**: the level the claim is about is a dashed rule named on itself before the trace starts,
  so the viewer sees the line cross it. Its name is placed along the rule where the line is not, above or below —
  checked between readings, not only at them.
- **A turning point is marked once passed** (« pic de 1973 »), never before its data.
- **The last reading is the subject**: ringed and named when the trace lands; the video ends on the whole line.
- The plot leaves room at the right for the widest tip label and at the left for the value ticks (floor, the reference,
  top — the static's three); the credit goes in the first corner of the plot the line leaves free.
