# Calendar heatmap — in video

Worked example: `proof/video-calendar-heatmap-geneva` (validated 2026-09-14), from `proof/static-calendar-heatmap-geneva`.

- **The gesture only a video has is letting the year happen**: every day takes its bin's colour in calendar order, linear
  in days (a time axis, never eased), so the season arrives as a band before any word points at it. A count climbs with
  the days it counts, each one counted once its cell has filled.
- **The run is traced, not boxed**: the days outside the claim step back to a neutral, and the outline runs along the run a
  day at a time — one rectangle per month it crosses — while its length is counted. The outline is the ink over a halo of
  the ground (every halo drawn before every stroke), so it parts from the darkest bin it runs along. The colours come
  back before the end, the outline kept.
- **The counts and the key share the band over the calendar**, no plate: the counts at the left, the swatches and their
  breaks (the unit on the last) at the right.
- **No close-up when the cell is large enough**: at 1920 × 1080 a day is about 50 pixels and a run reads at the year's scale.
- Before the fill, an empty day and a date that does not exist are the same pale step off the ground; the impossible dates
  stay pale when the year fills, so they need no label.
