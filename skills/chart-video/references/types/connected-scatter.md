# Connected scatter — in video

Worked example: `proof/video-connected-scatter-lowcarbon` (validated 2026-09-14), from `proof/static-connected-scatter-lowcarbon`.

- **The gesture only a video has is making the move happen**: each entity leaves its hollow ring and slides along its
  arc to its filled disc, the arc drawn as far as the disc has gone. Two dates and nothing between them: each journey is
  eased (an arrival), and the entities leave one after another so a count can climb as each one lands — the count is the
  number of discs landed, never a number interpolated on the clock.
- **The key is the ring and the disc with their two dates**, no plate, in the panel with the counters, seated in the
  corner of the plot no mark reaches at any scale the camera takes.
- **A crowd near the origin gets the close-up**: the x domain closes (seats and arcs laid out again at every frame, so a
  disc stays a disc), the entities outside leave the frame, the crowd is named. Names are seated twice in Bun — once per
  scale — and carried by their disc; one set of ticks at a time. The camera pulls back before the end.
- **The filter comes after the proof**: the entities the claim is about are picked out one after another on the whole
  axis, their count climbing; nothing is picked out before. The subject's arc splits into its two moves, each leg with
  its value, instead of a sentence.
- Names: the subject first (its seat must also hold at its first-date ring, where it is named from the start), then the
  picked, then the rest — beside the disc, then pushed with a hairline leader that crosses no word, disc or leg, then the
  code; at the whole scale an unpicked entity that finds no seat beside its disc stays unnamed.
