# Streamgraph — in video

Worked example: `proof/video-streamgraph-swiss-electricity` (validated 2026-09-14), from
`proof/static-streamgraph-swiss-electricity`.

- **The gesture only a video has is carrying a changing quantity along the time**: the stream is revealed by a front
  travelling linearly in years (a clip on the still's own stack — silhouette offset, inside-out order), and the tracked
  layer's name rides the front on its own band with the value that changes (here its rank that year, computed in Bun).
- The large bands are named inside themselves once the front has passed them, at their thickest point measured across
  the label's whole width; no value axis (a free baseline has none).
- The subject: every other band steps back and the year the claim is about is a rule through the stream — band names are
  seated clear of it.
- The y domain is padded so the stream has frame around it for the rule's year and the credit.
