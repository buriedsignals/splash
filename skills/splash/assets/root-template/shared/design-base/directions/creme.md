## Ground and accent

- `ground: #FFFCEE` — pixel route, modal colour, **93.0 % coverage**. A warm paper, not white.
- `accent: #1757B6` — pixel route, the single hue cluster at 216°, carrying its own lighter tints;
  the palette reads **sequential**, one pole.
