The answer recorded for this beat: the newsroom's own house colours, as they stand in
`skills/splash/assets/root-template/NEWSROOM.example.md` (`brandColor: "#0B7A75"`,
`ground: "#FFFFFF"`). `origin: newsroom` says who chose them.

**Minard's three tones are a category this beat does not have.** His plate separates beef, veal and
mutton, and names them in the key with their own colours. Here every band carries one quantity —
people under temporary protection — so the field is one colour, the direction's accent, and the
subject band is the accent at full strength while the rest are a tint of it.

**The water convention is declined, for the reason the contour beat records.** Nothing on this plate
is measured in blue, but the bands are drawn over the sea as much as over the land, and a blue sea
under a coloured band is a second tone competing with the one that carries the quantity. The sea is
the direction's bare ground; the land is one step off it, which is what
`the-basemap-gives-up-its-contrast` asks for on a plate whose ink belongs to the flow.
