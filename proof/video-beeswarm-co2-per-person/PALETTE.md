The answer recorded for this beat: the newsroom's own house colours, as they stand in
`skills/splash/assets/root-template/NEWSROOM.example.md` (`brandColor: "#0B7A75"`,
`ground: "#FFFFFF"`). `origin: newsroom` says who chose them.

**The reference this form is filed against spends colour on a category, and this beat does not.**
ABC's 8 500 schools are coloured by sector — three licensed hues that are the ABC's own. This plate
has no category to carry: every circle is a country, and the only channels in play are position
(tonnes per person) and area (population). So the field is one colour, the direction's own accent,
and `the-subject-is-ringed-not-recoloured` does the rest: the two called-out countries keep the
field's colour and gain a stroke.

That is the reference's own rule read back at it — *ring the called-out mark, do not recolour it,
when colour is already carrying a category* — applied to a plate where colour carries nothing. A hue
spent on a category this beat does not have would be a legend a reader has to learn for nothing.
