# Beat — Les 6 pays au-dessus de 20 t de CO₂ par personne pèsent 0,6 % de l'humanité (video)

**Type:** beeswarm (chart). **Medium/format:** chart / **video**. **Size:** landscape (1920 × 1080).

Same subject, same frozen file (`../static-beeswarm-co2-per-person/data.csv`) and the same assertions as
`proof/static-beeswarm-co2-per-person`: CO₂ per person in 2023 for 213 countries, one circle per country, its area its
population; the six countries above 20 t hold under 1 % of humanity (0,6 %); the world average (4,6 t, weighted by
population) sits above what more than 60 % of people emit; the largest circle, India, sits below the country median. One
colour for the field, the cases ringed, not recoloured.

## The argument

Humanity starts as one disc — every person, one area scale — sitting at the world average; it bursts into its 213
countries, each flying to its own tonnes and keeping its area, so the viewer sees where the people actually are; then a
copy of each of the six countries beyond 20 t flies into the outline of that same disc and merges: a speck of 0,6 %.

## The picture — shots, not a page

1. **The title card** (from frame 0, 1.5 s).
2. **The story** — the value axis at the foot, the world average as a rule, the swarm on the midline, the count at the
   top right while the countries land, India and China named on their circles, the tail bracketed from below.
3. **No end card** — the video ends on the whole swarm, the six beyond 20 t ringed and bracketed « 6 pays / 0,6 % », the
   credit on one line.
