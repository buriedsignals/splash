The newsroom's house colours.

**Two ramps and a neutral, all three built from the direction's own three colours.** The filed
treatment `the-ramp-deepens-outward` asks for one ramp per side, palest at the centre and deepest at
the extreme; jbryer's Likert plate picks brown against teal **for CVD separation, not for
prettiness**, and a beat with one house accent reaches the same separation another way:

- the **fossil** side ramps from the furniture's muted step toward the ink — deepest at coal, the
  outermost level;
- the **renewable** side ramps from a pale tint of the accent to the accent at full strength —
  deepest at wind, the outermost level;
- **nuclear**, which belongs to neither, is the achromatic neutral straddling the centre.

The two sides therefore differ in chroma as well as in lightness, which is what CVD separation
actually requires, and nothing is imported. That is `METHOD.md` correction 28 again: derive from the
direction, and where categories are ordered — and on this form they are, by definition, ordered
outward from the centre — ramp rather than enumerate.
