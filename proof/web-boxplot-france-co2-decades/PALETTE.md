The answer recorded for this beat: the newsroom's own house colours, as they stand in
`skills/splash/assets/root-template/NEWSROOM.example.md` (`brandColor: "#0B7A75"`,
`ground: "#FFFFFF"`). `origin: newsroom` says who chose them. This is the palette
`composeDirections` reconciles the three filed directions against; the delivered page is drawn in
whichever direction governs it, never in this one.

**One hue, and the type sheet is why.** `chart-beat/references/types/boxplot.md` allows a second hue
only when two groups are being deliberately compared side by side, and never more than two. This
plate compares ONE group — France — across eight decades of itself, so it spends one hue: the
direction's own accent, at full chroma on the peak decade and mixed toward the direction's ground
for the seven others. That is one hue at two chromas, the same shape
`two-states-of-one-measure-are-one-hue-at-two-chromas` names, and here the two states are «the
decade the argument is about» and «the decades it is measured against», not two quantities.

**The median line is NOT the box's colour, and that is this type's own rule rather than a house
preference.** A median sits inside its own box, so a median drawn in a tint of the fill is two
colours clamped against each other on the same ground — measured elsewhere in this tree at 1,023:1,
which is no contrast at all. It is drawn in ink on every box except the peak's, where the box is at
full accent and the median is drawn in the **ground** instead: a knockout against a saturated fill
rather than ink on ink.

**The sample dots are ink, not accent.** The 75 year-dots beside the boxes are a step off the
direction's own ground toward its ink, pushed up to the WCAG non-text floor against that ground if
the step lands under it. They carry no argument of their own — they are the readings the box
summarises — so they take no chroma.

**The yardstick borrows no series colour.** `level.ts`'s control paints its chosen pill ink-on-ground
and rings the chosen decade's box in ink; the three rules it lays across the plot are drawn in ink on
a ground casing. Colour on this plate is already carrying «which decade the claim is about», and a
control that spent the accent would make the colour meaning «the peak» also mean «you clicked here».

Nothing else on the page is chromatic. The axis labels, the gridlines, the fitted axis's own floor
rule and the outlier ring's stroke are steps off the direction's own ground, computed by
`deriveFurniture` at render time and never written here as a literal.
