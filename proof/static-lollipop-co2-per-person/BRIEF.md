# Beat — La Chine a triplé son CO₂ par personne ; l'écart avec l'Américain moyen est passé de 7,5 à 1,7

**Type:** lollipop (chart). **Medium/format:** chart / **static**. **Size:** landscape
(1920 x 1080), pinned in the front matter above.

The first `lollipop` beat in this tree.

## It is not the dumbbell this tree already carries, and the difference is the claim

`proof/more-dumbbell-life-expectancy-gains` joins two states with a bar between them, and that bar is
the **gap**: a dumbbell answers *how far apart* and says nothing about how far either end is from
nothing. A lollipop pair draws each state as its own stem **from zero**, so the **levels** are the
first reading and the gap the second.

On this data that difference is the whole claim. *The two averages are now within a factor of two* is
a sentence about levels — 14.3 t against 8.6 t — and a dumbbell of the same two countries would draw
two bars of similar length and say nothing of the kind.

## The claim

**China's CO₂ per person tripled between 2000 and 2023 (2.9 → 8.6 t) while the American average fell
by a third (21.4 → 14.3 t): the ratio between them went from 7.5 to 1.7.** The six countries drawn
are 63.9 % of the world's emissions.

The plate refuses to render if the subject did not roughly triple, if the ratio did not fall from
above five to under two and a half while staying above one, or if the six do not carry most of the
world's total.

**The six are a computed rule, not a pick**: the countries with the largest total emissions in 2023,
where the total is the per-person figure times the population, both from the frozen file. The rule is
printed on the plate, under the pairs — Iran is in it, which a hand-picked list would probably have
left out.

## The three rules the reference gave, all taken

- **The earlier state is a lighter tint of the later state's own hue** — *"not grey, not a second
  hue: the same hue, lighter"*, so a pair reads as one subject in two states rather than as two
  subjects. That is `two-states-of-one-measure-are-one-hue-at-two-chromas`, already carried on
  Datawrapper's and Statista's evidence, and this record makes **three desks**. The tint is taken as
  far toward the ground as it can go while still clearing the non-text floor against it — a past
  nobody can see is not a state, it is an absent mark.
- **The direction of change gets a glyph before it gets a number**, so the sign survives a glance.
  The triangle is **drawn as a path rather than set as `▲`**: U+25B2 is not guaranteed on any face of
  this base's family ladders, and a glyph the ladder cannot cover refuses every family and takes the
  whole plate down with it. A path costs nothing and always renders.
- **The change is a third row that belongs to the pair**, on the pair's own centreline, between the
  lollipops and the name — so it cannot be read as belonging to one of the two dates. The dates
  themselves sit under their own stems, small, in the furniture's ink: they are the axis, and they
  are treated as axis.

What the reference does with **colour identity** is not taken and `PALETTE.md` says why: it gives
each country its own hue, and six hues would be a palette this base does not own. The chroma axis is
spent on the two dates, so the subject cannot also take it —
`the-subject-is-ringed-not-recoloured` rings China's 2023 head instead.

`every-bar-labelled-lets-the-axis-go` — both values are printed above their own heads, as the
reference does, so the plate carries a zero line and its unit rather than a full value axis.

## The floor, and the row that had to be reserved

**A lollipop is read as a height from zero, and a height a reader cannot compare is a dot on a line.**
The stems owe six value-bands: room for the tallest stem, the number above its head, and enough
between six pairs to rank them by eye. The ladder spends copy until that is met.

Under the baseline the plate reserves **four** rows, not three: the dates, the change, the name — and
the selection rule. Drawn from the reading line's own baseline instead, the rule landed on the names.
A plate that says which six it chose has to reserve the line that says it.

## A note on this form's own evidence

One publication — Ferdio's #3, from an archive this base already marks as site-chrome contaminated,
and whose own pixel figures the record refuses to quote. Below the floor of two, so the form files
**no family rules of its own**; its three transferable moves are taken as imports, and the colour rule
it shares with two other desks is the one that was already filed.

## Source

series (2023) on the ISO code, frozen beside this beat as `data.csv` — 213 countries, of which the
plate draws the six the rule selects.
