# Beat — L’Espagne a ajouté plus d’électricité bas-carbone que la France depuis 2000 (video)

**Type:** stacked bar (chart). **Medium/format:** chart / **video**. **Size:** landscape (1920 × 1080).

Same subject, same frozen file (`../static-stacked-bar-lowcarbon-growth/data.csv`, Ember / Energy Institute via Our World
in Data) and the same derivation as `proof/static-stacked-bar-lowcarbon-growth`: low-carbon electricity (renewables and
nuclear) in 2000 and 2024, stacked `[level, growth]`, the twelve that added most of sixteen. Asserted, as the static does:
Spain added the most, more than France; France started several times higher; France still has the largest total.

## The argument, in one sentence

France's 2000 level is five copies of Spain's laid end to end — yet once the parts added since are pulled off their
levels and set side by side from zero, Spain's is the longest and France's only the seventh.

## The picture — shots, not a page

1. **The title card** (from frame 0, 1.5 s).
2. **The story** — twelve rows: the name at the left, the 2000 level in the pale tint from zero, the part added by 2024
   in the full accent stacked on it, the gain in a column at the right, the ticks under; over them the unit and a key of
   two swatches.
3. **No end card** — the video ends on the whole stack sorted by gain, Spain and France ringed; the credit on one line.
