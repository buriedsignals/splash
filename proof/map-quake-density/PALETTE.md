The answer recorded for this beat: the white ground and the vermillion the map already draws the
densest cell's ring in.

`origin: journalist`. It is not Heidi.news's house pair, and no subject convention produces it —
`matchConvention` reads "earthquake density" and returns nothing, because the four grounded
conventions are renewables, fossil, water and heat, and none of them fires on seismicity. The
vermillion was a choice this beat made; `journalist` is the honest word for a choice somebody made
that neither the newsroom nor a reader's own convention handed down.

It now carries the WHOLE hex field, not only the ring. The cell shading is the one thing a reader
reads a count off, and it used to be derived between the ground and the ink — grey, whatever the
newsroom recorded. `render.mjs` beside this file passes the accent to `dataRampEnd`, so the ramp
runs from the ground toward this colour and the map changes when the recorded answer changes.
