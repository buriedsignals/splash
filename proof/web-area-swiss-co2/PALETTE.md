The answer recorded for this beat: the newsroom's own house colours, as they stand in
`skills/splash/assets/root-template/NEWSROOM.example.md` (`brandColor: "#0B7A75"`,
`ground: "#FFFFFF"`). `origin: newsroom` says who chose them. This is the palette
`composeDirections` reconciles the three filed directions against; the delivered page is drawn in
whichever direction governs it, never in this one.

**One hue, two chromas, and no imported second hue.** The page partitions one quantity into two
halves of itself — the emissions before and after ONE year — and
`two-states-of-one-measure-are-one-hue-at-two-chromas` is exactly that case. Both halves are the
direction's own accent; the earlier one is a tint of it, mixed toward the direction's ground only as
far as the non-text floor against that ground allows.

**The year the cut falls on became the READER'S, and the colour rule did not move with it.** The
untouched page cuts at the year the cumulative total reaches its midpoint, which is the plate's own
cut; the yardstick (`chart-web/assets/level.ts`) re-cuts the same surface at a birth year the reader
picks, and every option's partition is drawn in the same two chromas of the same hue. That is why
the rule survives the control rather than being broken by it: two states of one measure stay two
states of one measure wherever the boundary between them is put, and a second hue would have turned
"before/after" into two categories at every position of the cut.

**The accent is spent once, so the reader's own rule is not drawn in it.** The accent carries the
SURFACE, which is the subject; the reference the reader stands up says "the year you asked about",
which is a different argument, so it is drawn in ink over a ground casing. The component measures
that pair against every fill the reference crosses — both chromas of the surface, the gridlines, the
midpoint rule and the bare ground — and refuses rather than draw a rule the picture swallows.

Nothing else on the page is chromatic. The axis labels, the rule at the midpoint year, the baseline
and the gridlines are steps off the direction's own ground, computed by `deriveFurniture` at render
time and never written here as a literal.
