The answer recorded for this beat: the newsroom's own house colours, as they stand in
`skills/splash/assets/root-template/NEWSROOM.example.md` (`brandColor: "#0B7A75"`,
`ground: "#FFFFFF"`). `origin: newsroom` says who chose them. This is the palette
`composeDirections` reconciles the three filed directions against; the delivered page is drawn in
whichever direction governs it, never in this one.

## One hue, and the type's filed defect is about exactly this

`chart-beat/references/types/beeswarm.md` files one thing that goes wrong for this type, and it is a
colour decision: *a beeswarm drawing a single undivided distribution has exactly one colour channel
to spend, and it has shipped on a default hue that had nothing to do with the subject.*

This page draws a single undivided distribution — 213 countries, no category split — so that sheet
applies in full. The channel is spent on **the two derived cases and nothing else**, and the field is
left achromatic:

- **the field** — 211 countries, a step off the direction's own ground (`mix(ground, ink, 0.34)`),
  raised to the non-text floor if that step lands under it. It is not a hue anyone chose, on purpose:
  `the-distribution-is-furniture-and-the-case-is-ink`.
- **the two cases** — the largest circle and the farthest one out, in the direction's own accent,
  calibrated against that direction's ground. Both are derived from the frozen file rather than
  picked, both are named in the headline's prose and in the alt text, and both are drawn in **every
  state of the control**.

**And that last word is where the control re-opened the type's trap in a form the static sibling
never met.** "The largest circle" is India only while a circle's area is population. Under *le CO₂*
the eye lands on China; under *les pays* there is no largest circle at all. Letting the accent follow
each weighting's own biggest mark is the obvious move and it is refused: `web-discipline.md` requires
that the accent on the subject be drawn unconditionally, so a page whose claim names India may not
let India go grey when a reader touches a control. What the refusal buys is the argument made
visible — the accented circle that is the largest object on the plate under *les habitants* is
exactly half the radius of an unaccented neutral one under *le CO₂*, measured: **27,1 against 66,4
geometry units.** China's own number is named in that option's revealed sentence, which is where a
reading a reader asked for belongs.

## No opacity anywhere on a mark, and that is a measurement

This page painted its calibrated field at `fill-opacity: 0.8` until the render was measured. The
colour a reader receives is then that fill **composited onto the ground** — a different colour, never
measured, which is the exact shape of the defect this branch has already paid for twice (1,75:1 and
2,19:1 on two beats whose accent was calibrated and then faded). A packed field has no overlaps by
construction: the packer guarantees a positive gap between every pair of circles, so the opacity was
buying nothing at all in exchange for making every number below wrong. It is gone, and every number
below is the colour the page actually paints.

## What a circle becomes under the pointer, and the dose is searched rather than typed

The mark itself answers — no dot is plastered over it, which the owner refused three times — and it
answers by **darkening off its own fill**, toward the direction's ink. The dose is not a constant: a
fixed step was refused elsewhere on this branch at 1,104:1 on `nocturne`, so here the smallest step
that clears **1,14:1 against that exact fill** is searched for and taken. There are two fills, so
there are two generated rules, and neither is an inline style, which would beat every selector.

On `nocturne` the search runs the other way — toward a lighter mint on a dark ground — and lands at
exactly 1,14:1, which is the floor doing its job rather than a number anybody chose.

## Nothing else is chromatic

The gridlines, the two reference rules, the baseline, the control's pills and its revealed sentences
are steps off the direction's own ground, computed by `deriveFurniture` at render time and never
written here as a literal. The two callouts' hairline leaders are the case colour, because they lead
to a case.
