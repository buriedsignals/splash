`palette`'s subject-fit branch was checked and it FIRES here, which is unusual in this corpus and
worth writing down. `matchConvention` holds grounded conventions for renewables, fossil fuel, water
and heat, and this beat's spokes are exactly those substances — nine sources of electricity. The
conventions were still not taken, for a reason the type forces: on a radar every spoke shares one
radial scale and the hue belongs to the ITEM, not to the axis. Colouring by source would mean nine
hues on a plate whose whole read is the shape of two polygons, and the two countries would then have
no colour left to be told apart by.

So the accent identifies FRANCE, the subject of the headline, and Germany takes the furniture's own
muted step. One accent, one neutral, two shapes — which is what `references/types/radar.md` asks
for when it caps a radar at about three items and requires the fills be transparent enough to see
where two shapes diverge.
