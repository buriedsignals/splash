The answer recorded for this beat: the newsroom's own house colours, as they stand in
`skills/splash/assets/root-template/NEWSROOM.example.md` (`brandColor: "#0B7A75"`,
`ground: "#FFFFFF"`). `origin: newsroom` says who chose them.

**The reference gives each entity its own hue and this beat cannot.** Ferdio's #85 carries three
countries in three house colours, one per entity, the same colour on both strips — which is
`colour-belongs-to-the-entity-not-to-the-state` met cleanly. Sixteen entities would need sixteen
hues, and METHOD correction 28 forbids inventing a palette this base does not own.

So the rule survives in the only form that scales: **nothing about a mark's colour changes between
the two strips.** All sixteen are the field's own tone on both, and the accent is spent on the one
country the headline is about — `context-in-neutral-at-the-subject-scale`. A reader can still see
that a mark on the upper strip and a mark on the lower one are the same country, because the chip
carries its name and the leader joins them.
