## The static plate's rules, and what the fluid frame asks of them

- **Every column carries its own number**, so no value axis; the zero baseline is the only rule.
- **The subject in the accent, the nine others one muted step off the ground.**
- **The set the headline adds up is drawn as a set**: bracketed, its sum on the bracket, and the rule at
  the subject's level stops where the set stops.
- **Names are never rotated and never cut.** On a frame too narrow for ten named columns — a phone —
  the same marks are drawn as rows (name, bar, value; the rule vertical, the bracket beside the set).
  Which orientation shows is measured in the reader's own pixels: every name must fit its column in
  two lines, every value its step, and the sum its bracket.
- **In rows, the sum sits on a chip of the ground** and the rule passes behind it; squeezed between
  bracket and rule it set one word a line.
- **The title steps down a ladder** until the fixed header fits its share of the frame.
