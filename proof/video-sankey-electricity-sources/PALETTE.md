The answer recorded for this beat: the newsroom's own house colours, as they stand in
`skills/splash/assets/root-template/NEWSROOM.example.md`.

`palette`'s subject-fit branch fires — the nodes are energy sources, and `matchConvention` holds
grounded conventions for renewables, fossil fuel, water and heat — and it is refused for a reason
this form makes explicit. Three of this family's four references measured the same arrangement:
**the flows nobody is tracking take the neutral, and the neutral is the largest area on the plate**
(LLNL's rejected energy at 11 % of the picture, the IEA's `#A1A1A1` at 5.31 % against a largest hue
at 4.25 %, Carbon Brief's 16 % neutral ribbon against 1 % accent). Nine source hues would spend the
entire palette on the furniture and leave nothing for the argument.

So: one accent, on the flow the headline is about, and every other ribbon in the furniture's own
muted step. `the-neutral-is-the-largest-area` is a filed treatment, not a preference, and this
palette is what obeying it looks like.
