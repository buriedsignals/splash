# Beat — Dans 5 de ces 6 pays l'éolien devance le solaire, la Suisse est l'exception (video)

**Type:** grouped bar (chart). **Medium/format:** chart / **video**. **Size:** landscape (1920 × 1080).

Same subject, same frozen file (`../static-wind-vs-solar/data.csv`, Ember / Energy Institute via Our World in Data) and the
same derivation as `proof/static-wind-vs-solar`: wind's and solar's shares of six countries' own electricity in 2024,
ordered by the gap between them. Asserted: six countries, solar ahead in exactly one, Switzerland.

## The picture — shots, not a page

1. **The title card** (from frame 0, 1.5 s).
2. **The story** — six groups across the frame, a name under each, a share over each bar; over them, the two series named
   in their own inks with the unit said once, and the lead count at the right. No plate, no axis — every bar is labelled.
3. **No end card** — the video ends on the six groups, Switzerland alone kept; the credit.

## The choreography

What only a video can do is **run the comparison one country at a time**: wind first across the six, then solar beside it
group by group, and the viewer watches solar land under wind five times before it towers over it once.
