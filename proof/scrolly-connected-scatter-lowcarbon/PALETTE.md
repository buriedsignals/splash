The answer recorded for this beat: the newsroom's own house colours, as they stand in
`skills/splash/assets/root-template/NEWSROOM.example.md` (`brandColor: "#0B7A75"`,
`ground: "#FFFFFF"`). `origin: newsroom` says who chose them.

**The reference gives every entity its own hue; this beat cannot, and would not.** Ferdio's plate
carries three countries and paints each one — a red, a blue, a third. Sixteen entities cannot be
given sixteen hues without inventing a palette this base does not own, which is exactly what METHOD
correction 28 forbids: every colour on a directed plate derives from the direction's own three.

So identity is carried by **position and by the label**, which is where it belongs on a scatter, and
colour carries the one thing the headline argues about: the subject at full accent, the other fifteen
in a tint of it. That is `accent-marks-the-thread` and
`context-in-neutral-at-the-subject-scale`, and it is also the only reading of the reference's colour
rule that survives contact with sixteen entities.

The hollow ring and the filled disc are one colour by construction — they are two states of one
entity, not two entities.
