## The argument

The world's CO₂ in 2000 is one ring whose circumference is its 24,7 Gt, the six countries laid end to end on it; the
ring grows to 37,0 Gt around the 2000 ring, each country's arc keeping its tonnes on the same length scale — China's
runs to a third of the turn, the United States' falls to about a seventh; then the world splits into its six countries, each
pair of arcs keeping its angle — one turn is the world on every ring — and the video ends on the six rings.

## The picture — shots, not a page

1. **The title card** (from frame 0, 1.5 s).
2. **The world** — one ring at the centre of the frame, the two years concentric, « Chine » and « États-Unis » named
   beside their arcs, the world's tonnes in the hole.
3. **No end card** — the video ends on the six rings side by side, the 2023 share in each hole, the name and both years'
   tonnes under each ring, China's number ringed, the credit on one line.

The static draws 2000 as the outer arc; here 2023 is the outer arc, because it is the ring that grew out of the 2000 one
and the split keeps each arc on its own side.
