**This beat's palette is a filed treatment rather than a decision.**
`two-states-of-one-measure-are-one-hue-at-two-chromas` comes from two publications that reached it
independently — Datawrapper ("value and target as one hue at two chromas... the reader is told these
are two states of the same quantity before reading a single label") and Statista ("the tint of the
same hue, not a second hue, for the earlier state. Two states of one measure should not read as two
categories"). So 2015 and 2024 are two chromas of the DIRECTION's own accent, and the track behind
them is the furniture's own neutral.

Nothing is imported. This is the smallest case of `METHOD.md` correction 28 — two marks, one
measure, one hue — and here the corpus states the rule rather than a repair discovering it.

`palette`'s subject-fit branch was checked: `matchConvention` fires on "renouvelables", and it is
refused for the same reason it is refused across this corpus's energy beats — a convention is the
chart's SINGLE accent, and this plate's accent is already spent on the measure, at two chromas.
