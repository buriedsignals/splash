## The static plate's rules, and what the fluid frame asks of them

- **The track runs the full scale**, so the remainder is legible; **two states, one hue at two chromas**,
  the thin saturated bar in front of the thick pale one; **every row prints its verdict**; **the states
  are named on the first row's own marks**, not in a legend.
- **The rows are a grid** — name, track, verdict — so the track takes whatever width is left and every
  length is a percentage of it. No second orientation is needed: the form is already rows.
- **The two state names are seated in the reader's own pixels.** On a phone the two marks are thirty
  pixels apart; the 2024 name takes a line of its own above when it would touch the 2015 name, and the
  2015 name starts at the track when it would run past it.
- **The title steps down a ladder of three forms** until the fixed header fits its share of the frame.
