The newsroom's house colours, as they stand in
`skills/splash/assets/root-template/NEWSROOM.example.md`.

Nine bands, one hue. The layers are stacked in a stated order — largest through the middle, thin ones
tapering outward, which is Lee Byron's inside-out — so their positions are ORDERED, and an ordered set
takes a sequential ramp of the direction's own accent rather than nine categorical hues (`METHOD.md`
correction 28). The band the headline is about takes the full accent; UNHCR's own rule that the
residual gets no hue falls out of the same arrangement, because the thin outer layers sit at the pale
end of the ramp.

Nothing is imported. On a form with no value axis the fills are doing identity work that no legend
can take back, so every one of them has to be a colour the direction set.
