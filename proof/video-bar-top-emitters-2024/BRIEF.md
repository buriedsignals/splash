## The choreography — an argument, not a reveal

The still ranks the ten. The video **does the arithmetic from the world down**: the world's emissions as one bar, the ten
largest inside it; the ten falling out into their rows, each keeping its length; the camera closing onto them; the five
after China lining up under it and stopping short — and the tenth, Germany, sliding into what is left, with room to spare.
