# Beat — L'Allemagne a produit 143 TWh d'électricité de moins en 2024 qu'en 2015 (video)

**Type:** waterfall (chart). **Medium/format:** chart / **video**. **Size:** landscape (1920 × 1080).

Same subject, same frozen file (`../static-germany-electricity-bridge/data.csv`, Ember and Energy Institute via Our World in
Data) and the same assertions as `proof/static-germany-electricity-bridge`: Germany's generation in 2015 and 2024 grouped
into renewables, nuclear and fossil; the bridge replayed (opening + every step = closing, to the tenth); renewables rose,
nuclear and fossil fell, the net change is a fall of 143 TWh. One scale from zero for the totals, the members and the steps.

## The argument

The waterfall's steps are not readings: they are what separates two mixes — so the video opens both totals into their
members and pulls out of them, keeping every length, the part 2024 gained and the parts 2015 lost, which fall into place as
the bridge.

## The picture — shots, not a page

1. **The title card** (from frame 0, 1.5 s).
2. **The story** — the five slots on the whole frame, the ticks at the left; the names beside the 2015 bar while it is a
   mix, under their slots once they are steps.
3. **No end card** — the video ends on the whole waterfall, the net change bracketed between the two totals; the credit
   on one line. About 18.5 s.
